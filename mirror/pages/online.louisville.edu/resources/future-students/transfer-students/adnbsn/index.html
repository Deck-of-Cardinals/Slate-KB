Enroll in UofL’s RN-BSN while getting your ADN

Students enrolled in the Associate Degree in Nursing (ADN) at KCTCS or IvyTech may concurrently start in UofL’s online RN to BSN program after successfully completing their first semester at their home-base institution.

Prepare for concurrent enrollment at UofL - See admissions requirements for KCTCS and IvyTech.

  • Take the RN license exam (NCLEX) after completing your ADN
  • Graduate with your RN-BSN degree months earlier, rather than waiting to complete your ADN first or starting work as a nurse to begin the online RN to BSN program
  • Complete a Professional Nursing Portfolio (30 credit hours awarded) and save $11,250 in tuition
  • Move through the curriculum one course at the time with accelerated 8-week terms
