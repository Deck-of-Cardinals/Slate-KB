Bachelor of Arts in Political Science, Online

Designed for working professionals looking to start or advance their career in political science by gaining expertise in areas related to policy, politics and governments in the United States as well as around the world.

The online Bachelor of Arts in Political Science program at UofL cultivates an understanding of policy, politics and governments in the United States and around the globe. A wide range of courses including campaigns and elections, the Presidency and Congress, social movements and civil rights, terrorism and war, constitutional law, environmental policy, religious politics, urban politics and poverty and international politics, allows you to focus and customize your degree experience.

Learn how these areas impact our society, government, education system, workplaces and communities – then prepare to leave your mark in the field of political science.

U.S. News & World Report recognition

The online Bachelor of Arts in Political Science (BAPOLS) is offered by the College of Arts & Sciences at the University of Louisville. For over a decade, UofL Online bachelor’s degree programs have been consistently recognized as "Best in the Nation" and "Best for Veterans," by U.S. News & World Report, reflecting the university’s focus on academic excellence and student support.

The Big Picture

With an emphasis on research, data collection, critical analysis and clear communication in writing and speaking, BAPOLS graduates will be prepared for a variety of careers in the public, private and non-profit sectors, as well as ready to pursue advanced education. UofL Online graduates go on to make an impact through roles in government organizations, political campaigns, the nonprofit sector, law, public policy, academics and business, both in the U.S. and abroad.

Online BA in Political Science Degree Highlights

  • Explore how issues related to power, conflict and justice within our political systems, policies, government and society impact future change.
  • Earn a highly marketable and sought-after degree in as little as two years with transfer credits—on your time and schedule with 100% online classes.
  • Complete coursework in the four major subfields of political science (American politics, comparative politics, international relations and political theory) and take supporting courses in specific topics like campaigns and elections, the Presidency and Congress, social movements and civil rights, terrorism and war, constitutional law, environmental policy, religious politics, urban politics and international politics.
  • Take advantage of internship opportunities wherever you are in the public, private or nonprofit sector.
  • Learn from distinguished university faculty with notable academic credentials, political science experience and a commitment to applied research.

  • Build a strong foundation for pursuing impactful careers, law degrees or graduate degrees in political science.

What Are Admissions Requirements for the Online BA in Political Science?

New students and transfer students with less than 24 transferable credits from accredited institutions must have:

  • High school diploma from an accredited institution (GED or equivalent also accepted)
  • Minimum high school GPA of 2.5 on a 4.0 scale; AND for transfer students, a minimum college GPA of 2.0 (applicants who do not meet the minimum GPA may inquire about conditional acceptance)
  • Composite ACT* score of 21 or higher (test optional admission is granted on a case-by-case basis)

Transfer students with 24 or more transferable credits from accredited institutions must have:

  • Minimum college GPA of 2.0 on a 4.0 scale (applicants who do not meet the minimum GPA may inquire about conditional acceptance)

*Your Application Portal:

Once you have started an undergraduate application, you can check the status and review any additional checklist to-do items. Log in to your application using the email address you used to apply for admission and your password. Your checklist items may include additional materials or documentation that facilitate a smooth admissions process. You will also have access to important contact information and next steps after an admissions decision is made.

No ACT/SAT? Test-optional admissions are granted on a case-by-case basis and require a comprehensive review of high school transcript, including grades and college-preparatory courses. A personal statement may be requested during the admissions review process.

Transfer Credit

Students may transfer up to 60 hours from a community college and up to 90 hours from an accredited four-year college. Transfer applicants must have a minimum college GPA of 2.0 to be admitted in good standing. Review the Transfer Evaluation System to get an idea of how many credits you'll be able to transfer in toward your UofL bachelor's degree, and visit our General Transfer Student Page for more details.

  • Are you a KCTCS student? KCTCS students can transfer completed associate degrees in their entirety and begin immediately with online upper level coursework at UofL. Visit our KCTCS Transfers Page for more information.

See the UofL Transfer Credit Policy for more information.

What You Learn in the Online Bachelor of Arts in Political Science

The online Bachelor of Arts in Political Science is a 121-credit hour program, offered 100% online. Courses are delivered asynchronously, in standard term length. UofL’s curriculum builds a strong foundation in American government, comparative politics and international relations while strengthening analytical, research and communication skills.

Online Courses 

Coursework emphasizes political institutions, public policy, global systems and data-informed inquiry, giving students the tools to evaluate complex issues, interpret evidence and construct persuasive arguments. Advanced courses integrate theory and applied research, ensuring students can move from understanding political systems to analyzing how they function in real-world contexts.
 

Curriculum Components

  • General Education (Cardinal Core) requirements: 31 credit hours
  • Arts & Sciences requirements: 22 credit hours
  • Department of Political Science requirements: 15 credit hours
  • Track requirements: 53 credit hours

What Can I Do With a Bachelor’s in Political Science?

Earning a degree in political science paves the way for graduates to pursue a variety of roles that go well beyond government and politics, while also developing critical thinking, research, writing and analytical skills. No matter what area of political science you study, the critical skills you develop in research, analysis and communications can help you succeed both inside and outside the political arena.

University of Louisville’s political science graduates have been extremely successful in earning prestigious scholarships and fellowships, including numerous Fulbright Award winners and multiple Truman Scholars. Many graduates go on to have impactful careers – both in the U.S. and abroad – in the following fields:

  • Local, state and federal government agencies
  • Election and political advocacy campaigns
  • International affairs
  • Economic development offices
  • Non-governmental organizations
  • Nonprofit organizations
  • Regulatory agencies
  • Law/legal organizations
  • Public policy
  • Business
  • Academia
  • Media

According to Lightcast occupation outlook data, jobs in the political science field are projected to grow, on average, 8% between 2025-2033, with 166,314 annual job postings. The median salary for political science-related jobs is nearly $80,800 (Lightcast.io). Career opportunities and earnings vary based on actual job role, geographic location, prior experience and employer. These figures reflect overall industry trends and are not a guarantee of individual results.   

Students who want to explore their career options with a bachelor's degree in political science can reach out to the UofL Career Development Center, which supports students from their freshman year to their senior year and beyond with career assessments, advising, job and internship postings, resume and interview help, special events and more.

What Else Should You Know?

The questions and answers below help you learn more about our program, delivery method, application and admission process, financial aid options and how to succeed as an online student at UofL and beyond. 

  • What can I do with this degree?

Many graduates go on to have successful careers, both domestically and abroad, in the following fields: local, state and federal government agencies; campaigns; international affairs; economic development offices; non-governmental organizations; nonprofit organizations; regulatory agencies; law/legal organizations; public policy; business; academia; and media. Graduates have also been extremely successful in earning prestigious scholarships and fellowships, including numerous Fulbright Award winners and multiple Truman Scholars.

  • How long will the program take to complete?

The online Bachelor of Arts in Political Science is a 121-credit hour program. The duration of the program depends on several factors, including previous academic work and credit transfer (associate degree or equivalent credit earned from an accredited institutions), course load per semester and your individual learning style and pace. Typical students coming in with an associate degree may only take two years to finish their bachelor’s degree at UofL.

  • Are the courses synchronous or asynchronous?

Courses are asynchronous, meaning that students can learn anytime and from anywhere. The online asynchronous course delivery provides flexibility and adaptability to individual’s learning needs, methods and life schedule.

  • Will I earn my degree from UofL?

Yes. The Bachelor of Arts in Political Science will be granted by the University of Louisville and represented as such on the diploma.

  • Is this program accredited?

Yes, the BA in Political Science program at UofL has the same accreditation as the university. The University of Louisville is accredited by the Commission on Colleges of the Southern Association of Colleges and Schools (SACSCOC).

  • Is this program suitable for me as an out-of-state student?

Yes, this program is open to students in other states. Also, online students outside Kentucky enrolled in fully online programs pay the same tuition as in-state students. To learn more about whether this program fits your needs, aspirations and goals, contact our enrollment counselors for more guidance.
