KCTCS Transfer Students

As a KCTCS-connected student, you have options for earning your bachelor's degree fully online at UofL. Transferring your credits earned at KCTCS toward a bachelor’s program at UofL means you can hit the ground running and get ahead faster.
 

Every day, students just like you achieve their goals with one of UofL's 100% online programs. Now it's your turn!

KCTCS/IvyTech Transfer Scholarship!

UofL’s KCTCS Academic Transfer Scholarship is offered to eligible students transferring from a KCTCS institution or the Ivy Tech Community College, Sellersburg.

Scholarship Application Open, Priority Deadline: March 1
Scholarship Application Open, Extended Deadline: April 15

Check Out the Degree Pathways and Courses Accepted at UofL

KCTCS courses indicated in the pathway are accepted at UofL and count toward completion of your bachelor’s degree. When you follow the pathway, no additional general education (Cardinal Core) courses are needed.

Transfer pathways lead to an Associate of Art, Associate of Science or Associate of Applied Science degree from Kentucky Community and Technical College Systems and set you up for a successful bachelor’s degree experience at UofL.

The following bachelor’s programs are available fully online at UofL and feature “pathways” for recommended coursework you can complete at KCTCS that seamlessly transfers when you are ready to start at UofL.

Preparing for Next Step at UofL while Enrolled at KCTCS

Planning your KCTCS coursework using a transfer pathway as a guide can help you complete your associates degree with an official transcript that is GenEd-Certified and aligned with the prerequisites for the bachelor’s degree you intend to pursue at the University of Louisville, 100% online.
