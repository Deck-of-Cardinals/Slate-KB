Master of Science in Materials and Energy Science and Engineering, 100% Online

Note: The Master of Science in Materials and Energy Science and Engineering will not be accepting new applications after the Fall term application deadline of August 1.

The online master’s in materials and energy science and engineering (MESE) is designed for professionals within the field of materials and energy science wanting to significantly advance their careers and develop the skillset needed to support and implement new energy technologies.

 

Meeting the rising energy demand is one of our greatest challenges, affecting quality of life, education and commerce across the globe. UofL’s online engineering master’s equips materials and energy scientists and engineers to better address this challenge and influence the quality of life worldwide. This multidisciplinary program delivers in-depth knowledge in areas such as materials chemistry and physics, processing, energy conversion and storage and systems-level engineering. 

The online master’s focused on materials and energy science and engineering is offered by the J.B. Speed School of Engineering, which has been consistently recognized by U.S. News & World Report for its Graduate Engineering Programs, academic excellence and student support.

The Big Picture

Our civilization faces growing energy consumption and subsequent demand challenges. Preparing our students and graduates to apply their knowledge to overcome this challenge through the creation and integration of new, advanced earth-abundant materials into cost-effective technologies lies at the heart of UofL’s energy and materials science online program.

This undertaking includes important tasks like redefining our dependence on fossil-based fuels and low-energy efficiency systems – thus lowering our carbon impact on the environment – as well as finding new ways of attaining renewable energy generation, such as tapping into the immense potency of the sun.

The online master’s in materials and energy science and engineering at the University of Louisville prepares engineering professionals to design, develop and enact potential solutions and technologies that advance the way energy is generated, stored and distributed. UofL’s curriculum enables students to better understand the broad social, ethical and professional issues related to contemporary science and engineering practices.

Online MS MESE Program Degree Highlights

  • Gain expertise and highly marketable skills in areas including solar energy conversion, energy storage, biofuels and biomass conversion, solar fuels, materials characterization and advanced energy materials.
  • Develop advanced-level knowledge in key materials and energy science, engineering and research areas.
  • Complete your degree in 18-months as a full-time student or enroll part-time and move through the program at your pace.
  • Take advantage of convenient 100% online classes with access to courses anytime, anywhere.
  • Build a strong foundation to pursue doctoral studies in related disciplines.

Systems Engineering Course/Project/Internship Requirement

To meet the master’s degree graduation requirements, students complete either a course or a project in systems engineering. 

  • For the course option, students can choose engineering entrepreneurship in renewable energy, techno-economic analysis and energy policy or smart manufacturing.
  • For the project option, students can work with a faculty or team leader on a renewable energy systems topic and produce a written report.
  • For the internship option, students can choose an external internship or practicum opportunity under the guidance of a faculty advisor.

What Are Admissions Requirements for the Online Master’s in Materials and Energy Science and Engineering
 

To be considered for admission, applicants must have:

  • A bachelor’s degree or its equivalent from an accredited institution
  • A minimum undergraduate GPA of 3.0 on a 4.0 scale (applicants who do not meet the minimum GPA may inquire about conditional acceptance) 

*Required application packet materials include:

  • Transcripts showing an undergraduate degree in engineering; degrees in chemistry or physics may also be considered
  • A written statement of interest describing what motivated you to apply to the MESE program, including previous experience in the field and how this degree will help you fulfill your career goals
  • Two letters of recommendation
  • Current resume

*Your application portal:

Once you have started the graduate application, you can check the status and review any additional checklist to-do items. Log in to your application using the email address you used to apply for admission and your password. Your checklist items may include additional materials or documentation that facilitate a smooth admissions process. What You Learn in the Online Master’s in Materials and Energy Science and Engineering

Online Courses

The online master of science in materials and energy science and engineering is a 30-credit-hour (10 course) program that requires 5 core MESE fundamental courses, 2 courses covering energy processing, 2 courses covering energy conversion and 1 systems engineering course or project. The program is 100% online. Courses are delivered asynchronously, in standard term length.

What Else Should You Know?

The questions and answers below help you learn more about our program, delivery method, application and admission, internship process and options or how to succeed as an online student at UofL and beyond. 

  • What bachelor’s degrees are acceptable for admission into the MS in Materials and Energy Science & Engineering (MESE) program?

A bachelor’s degree in engineering from an ABET-accredited program or an equivalent bachelor’s degree in physics or chemistry from an accredited university is required for admission into the MS in materials and energy science and engineering program.

  • Are GRE scores required for admission?

No, GRE scores are not required for admission into the MS MESE online program.

  • Is there a specific GPA requirement for admission into the program?

Yes, successful applicants typically have a 3.0 GPA (on a 4.0 scale) for previous coursework completed at accredited institutions. However, applicants can be admitted provisionally with a 2.75 GPA, but must maintain a 3.0 GPA in the program to graduate.

  • Can I receive credit for graduate courses completed at another institution?

Once admitted, students may request department permission to transfer up to six (6) credit hours from an accredited graduate program. Only courses that have not counted towards another degree program will be considered. See the Graduate School Transfer of Credit Policy for more information.

  • How quickly can I complete the online degree in MS MESE?

Full-time students can complete the MS MESE degree in just one year with 15-week terms. Students can also choose to enroll as a part-time student and move through the program at a pace that works best for them.

  • Is there an internship or project required for this degree?

The ME MESE online degree requires students to complete a system engineering course or project in systems engineering to satisfy the credits needed to complete the degree. Students can choose from courses in engineering entrepreneurship in renewable energy, techno-economic analysis and energy policy, smart manufacturing or a project-based course. If students choose to complete the project-based course, they will work with a faculty or team leader on a renewable energy systems topic and develop an in-depth understanding and provide a written report that includes problem definition, literature review, studies/analysis conducted and conclusions. The project can also be replaced with an external internship or practicum opportunity under the guidance of a faculty advisor.

  • What can I do with a master’s degree in materials and energy science & engineering

UofL’s master of science in materials and energy science and engineering prepares students for career tracks in industries including semiconductor/opto-electronics, catalysts and energy as well as to pursue entrepreneurship or join government and corporate labs. Graduates will also have a strong foundation to pursue doctoral studies in related materials and energy science and engineering disciplines. Some employment opportunities for graduates with this degree include semiconductor processing engineer, materials engineer, project manager, sustainability consultant, research and development engineer and so much more.
