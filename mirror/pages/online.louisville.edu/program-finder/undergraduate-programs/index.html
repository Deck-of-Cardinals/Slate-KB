Undergraduate Online Degrees

Online undergraduate programs at the University of Louisville are designed for motivated learners ready to move forward in their education and their careers. 

Whether you are starting your first degree, returning to finish what you began or seeking to build new skills, UofL Online offers flexible pathways that fit the realities of work and life. 

Our programs combine strong academic foundations with practical knowledge so you graduate prepared to grow professionally, adapt to change and pursue new opportunities.
