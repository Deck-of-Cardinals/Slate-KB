Student Achievement Metrics

Student success matters at UofL. The university sets clear goals and tracks progress using key metrics from its strategic plan.

Learn more about UofL's strategic plan
Explore all student data about

Key student achievement indicators

  • Six-year undergraduate graduation rates

    The percentage of full-time, first-time students who started a bachelors degree who graduate within six years

  • First to second year retention rates

    The percentage of full-time, first-year students who who started a bachelors degree that return to UofL for their second year

  • Degrees and certificates awarded

    The total number of degrees and certificates awarded each academic year

  • Professional licensure exam passage rates

    UofL students performance on professional licensure exams in audiology, dentistry, education, law, medicine, nursing, and speech-language pathology

Six-year undergraduate graduation rates

Key student completion indicator: UofL tracks the percentage of full-time, first-time bachelors degree-seeking students who graduate within six years (150% of normal time).

Professional licensure examinations

UofL collects student pass rate data for discipline-based licensure exams in fields like audiology, dentistry, education, law, medicine, nursing, and speech-language pathology. Academic units are responsible for gathering and using this data to meet program-level accreditation standards and support strategic planning.

Initial and advanced teacher preparation programs licensure exam pass rates

The College of Education and Human Development monitors the percentage of graduates who pass required licensure exams to become certified teachers or administrators in P–12 settings. This rate includes all exam attempts within two years of graduation and supports accreditation and program improvement.
