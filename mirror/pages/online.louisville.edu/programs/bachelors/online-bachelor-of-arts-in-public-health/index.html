Bachelor of Arts in Public Health, Online

UofL’s 100% online Public Health bachelor’s degree is designed for those who want to study the factors that influence health across entire populations, from human behavior and environmental conditions to health policy, health care systems, epidemiology and social determinants of health.

This degree is designed to help you build a broad understanding of public health while gaining practical skills in communication, critical thinking, data-informed decision-making, social and behavioral sciences and burden of disease in populations. Whether you want to begin a career in public health, to strengthen your preparation for a health-related profession or plan to continue into graduate school, UofL’s online BA in Public Health can help you take the next step.

This program is offered online by the School of Public Health & Information Sciences at the University of Louisville which has been consistently ranked among the best in the nation by U.S. News & World Report, recognized for its academic excellence and student support.

Bachelor of Arts in Public Health, 100% Online

The Big Picture

Public health professionals ask big questions. Why do some communities experience better health outcomes than others? How can disease and injury be prevented? What role do health policy, environmental conditions, behavior, access to care and social conditions play in determining and influencing health?

UofL’s online Bachelor of Arts in Public Health prepares you to explore those questions and understand the systems and strategies used to improve population health.

Through an interdisciplinary curriculum, you’ll build foundational knowledge across epidemiology, health policy, environmental health, health communication, quantitative methods, health care systems and public health interventions.

You’ll also develop transferable skills in critical thinking, communication and data-driven decision making that can support a variety of public health careers and graduate or professional pathways.

The BA in Public Health requires a minimum of 120 credit hours, including 47 credit hours of public health program requirements, supporting coursework, general education requirements and electives. Students can transfer in up to 60 hours of previously earned college credit from a community college and up to 90 credits from 4-year accredited institutions, making this bachelor’s degree attainable. 

Online BA in Public Health Degree Highlights

  • Build knowledge across key public health disciplines including epidemiology, health behavior, environmental health, health policy and quantitative methods
  • Explore how social, behavioral, biological and environmental factors affect population health
  • Learn how public health interventions can address community and population health needs
  • Develop skills in public health communication and data-informed decision-making
  • Apply what you’ve learned through a two-part senior capstone experience
  • Prepare for career opportunities in public health, health services, health education, policy, research and related fields
  • Build a foundation for graduate or professional education
  • Pursue an accelerated BA–MPH pathway if you meet eligibility requirements (please note, most MPH coursework is only offered in-person)

Public Health Starts With the Bigger Picture

Doctors, nurses and other clinicians often focus on the health of individual patients. Public health professionals impact communities more broadly by examining the systems, behaviors, environments and policies affecting the health of entire populations.

That broader perspective can prepare you to help communities prevent illness, promote healthier behaviors, understand health trends and improve health outcomes.

What Can I Do with a Bachelor’s in Public Health?

Public health is a broad field that connects health care, government, education, research, community services, nonprofit work, policy and communication.

UofL’s BA in Public Health prepares students for opportunities in public health, health services and public policy, including areas such as health education, health care administration, health policy and environmental health. Potential work settings include local and government health agencies and community or international organizations.

According to Lightcast and bls.gov, public health employment is projected to grow 24% through 2035, much faster than the average. The median annual wage for these occupations was $86,400 in 2025.

What Else Should You Know About a Bachelor’s Degree in Public Health?

Choosing an undergraduate degree is a major decision. These answers address some of the most common questions students ask when comparing public health bachelor’s degrees, career opportunities and online programs.

  • Is a BA in Public Health worth it?

A BA in Public Health can be valuable for students who want to build a career connected to population health, community health, health education, health services, research or policy, or who plan to pursue graduate or professional education. Public health is also a broad field, so the value of the degree depends partly on how you use your undergraduate experience. Internships, related work experience, networking, technical skills and graduate education can all expand your future opportunities. At UofL, the BA provides preparation for public health and health-services roles while also serving as a foundation for advanced study in public health and clinical health professions.

  • Is a BA or BS better for public health? Do employers have a preference between the two?

The best option depends on the curriculum and your academic and professional goals. A BA may provide broader interdisciplinary flexibility, while some BS programs may include additional science or quantitative requirements. Employers and graduate schools will generally consider your overall education, experience, skills and qualifications, not simply whether your degree says BA or BS. 

Employer requirements vary by position. For many entry-level public health roles, relevant coursework, professional experience, internships, communication abilities, analytical skills and knowledge of public health may matter more than whether your bachelor’s degree is classified as a BA or BS. Please review requirements for the specific career paths you are interested inand build relevant experience while completing your

  • Is a public health bachelor’s degree a good career path?

Public health can be a strong choice for professionals interested in improving health at a population or community level rather than working exclusively in direct patient care. The field includes opportunities related to community health, health education, policy, health services, research, environmental health and public-sector programs. Your individual career path will depend on your interests, skills, experience and whether you plan to pursue additional education.

  • What jobs can I get with a BA in Public Health?

Potential career areas can include:

  • Community health
  • Health education and promotion
  • Public health program coordination
  • Community outreach
  • Health communication
  • Health care administration
  • Health research
  • Environmental health
  • Government health programs
  • Health policy
  • Nonprofit and community services

Specific job titles and qualification requirements vary by employer and may require additional experience, certification or graduate education.

  • Do you need an MPH to work in public health?

Not for every position. A bachelor’s degree may qualify graduates for a range of entry-level and early-career opportunities. An MPH or another graduate degree may be required or preferred for more specialized positions, advanced analytical roles and leadership opportunities. Students interested in continuing their education can explore UofL’s accelerated BA–MPH option, which allows eligible students to begin graduate public health coursework while completing their bachelor’s degree.

  • What is the difference between public health and health care?

Health care is often focused on diagnosing and treating individual patients. Public health focuses more broadly on preventing illness, promoting health and improving outcomes across communities and populations. Public health professionals may study disease patterns, develop health education programs, evaluate community needs, analyze policy, investigate environmental risks or work to improve access to health resources.

  • Is this public health bachelor’s degree completely online?

Yes. UofL’s Bachelor of Arts in Public Health can be completed 100% online, asynchronously (access courses anytime convenient for you, your schedule and lifestyle, and complete assignments according with the specified due date).

  • How many credit hours is the online BA in Public Health?

The program requires successful completion of a minimum of 120 total credit hours, including 47 hours of public health core knowledge. Students may be able to transfer up to 90 credit hours to complete this degree faster. Speak with an enrollment counselor for more information. 

  • What do you study in a public health undergraduate program?

UofL’s public health undergraduate curriculum includes coursework in:

  • Epidemiology
  • Public health behavior
  • Public health interventions
  • Health policy
  • Health care delivery
  • Environmental health
  • Population health biology
  • Quantitative methods
  • Public health communication
  • Social epidemiology
  • Contemporary public health issues
  • Career readiness

Students also complete a two-part senior capstone.

  • Is public health the same as epidemiology?

No. Epidemiology is one important discipline within public health. Epidemiologists study patterns and causes of health outcomes within populations. A public health degree is broader and can include epidemiology alongside health behavior, environmental health, policy, health systems, communication, quantitative methods and other areas.

  • Can I transfer credits into the online public health program?

Students with previous college coursework can apply to UofL as transfer students. Students may transfer up to 60 hours from a community college and up to 90 hours from an accredited four-year college. ⁠Learn more about credit transfer opportunities, or speak with an enrollment counselor for more information. Students submit transcripts from all undergraduate institutions attended as part of the admissions process. Upon review, some or all credit may be applied to the degree program selected. How many credits apply toward the BA in Public Health will depend on your previous coursework and UofL’s transfer-credit evaluation.

  • What GPA do I need for the BA in Public Health?

For first-year students and transfer students with fewer than 24 college credit hours, admission in good standing to SPHIS generally requires a 2.5 high school GPA and a 2.0 college GPA if applicable. Transfer students with 24 or more college credit hours must meet UofL admission requirements, including a minimum 2.0 cumulative college GPA.

  • Do I need ACT or SAT scores?

Standardized test scores are optional under the program’s current admission requirements.

  • How much does the online BA in Public Health cost?

The current standard tuition rate for fully online undergraduate programs at UofL is $568 per credit hour, plus a $10 per-credit-hour online course fee. Eligible military students may qualify for a $250 per-credit-hour tuition rate for most undergraduate programs. Tuition and fees are subject to change.

  • Is financial aid available for online students?

Eligible online undergraduate students may qualify for federal financial aid and other forms of assistance, including grants, scholarships, loans, employer benefits and military education benefits.

  • How do I get started?

Apply for undergraduate admission to the University of Louisville or request information to connect with a UofL Online enrollment counselor.
