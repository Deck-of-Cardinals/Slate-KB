Getting Help & FAQs

Post-baccalaureate students have a bachelor's degree and want to take classes but are not pursuing an undergraduate degree at this time.

Many students take additional courses before applying to graduate school or to improve their cumulative GPA.

To Apply:

  1. Complete UofL's Take Flight Application.
  2. Pay your $30 application fee. If you received a bachelor's degree from UofL, no application fee is required.
  3. Submit your official degree-showing transcript(s). You do not need to submit a transcript if you received your bachelor's degree from the University of Louisville.
