Advantages

Paying for the rising cost of a college education is a concern for nearly every student and family. To help you meet your educational expenses, the University of Louisville is pleased to offer Nelnet Business Solutions (NBS) as a convenient budget plan. This is not a loan program. You have no debt, there are no interest or finance charges and there is no credit check. Enrollment in this plan is easy and available online.

Questions?

Contact NBS Customer Service Representatives 24/7 at 1-800-609-8056 or bursar@louisville.edu or 502-852-6503 during normal hours of operation.

How to Enroll

  1. Visit www.ulink.louisville.edu
  2. Select the "Financial Account" tile
  3. Select "Payment Plan"
  4. Setup Nelnet Payment Plan on Nelnet Portal

Payment plans are for CURRENT semester tuition/fees expenses ONLY.
For detailed information on deadlines, payment plan fees, down payments and other inquiries about the plan, please visit: mycollegepaymentplan.com or call 502-852-6503.
