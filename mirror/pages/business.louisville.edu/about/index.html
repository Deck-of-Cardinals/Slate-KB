About the College of Business

The College of Business at the University of Louisville enhances the intellectual and economic vitality of our city, the region and the broader business world through our academic programs, research and community outreach activities. We strongly believe that lives improve through entrepreneurship, innovation, critical thinking and people.

Connected to Louisville

UofL's College of Business is deeply and deliberately “of Louisville.” We build upon the city’s strengths and we contribute to those strengths in purposeful and reciprocal ways. We are committed to making all of Louisville and all of its citizens better as, at the same time, the city defines us and grounds our strategies and programs.
