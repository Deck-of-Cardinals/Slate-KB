Master of Engineering in Engineering Management, Online

The online master of engineering in engineering management (MEEM) degree is designed for experienced engineers who want to develop skills in designing solutions, managing engineering projects, products and services.

UofL’s online master of engineering in engineering management degree builds on your current knowledge and experience to expand the professional opportunities available to you. 

Gain the credentials and knowledge you need to take charge of complex engineering projects, play an active role in the development of your organization, move into leadership roles and increase your earnings. 

The MEEM program is delivered 100% online, giving you the flexibility you need to focus on one course at the time and balance work and life while earning a high-quality engineering education. 

The online MEEM program is an IISE Qualified Training Provider (QTP) and an ASEM Academic Partner. 
The J.B. Speed School of Engineering has been consistently recognized by U.S. News & World Report for its graduate engineering programs, academic excellence and student support.

The Big Picture

The online master of engineering in engineering management at the University of Louisville is a 30-credit-hours program built for professionals who want to expand their influence in administration and management without leaving the engineering discipline knowledge behind. 

This program is well suited for engineers who are ready to move from individual contribution to broader responsibility and seek to shape strategy, guiding teams and driving results in complex technical environments. It offers a clear path to leadership for those who want to connect engineering expertise with organizational impact and long-term career growth.

With high-quality courses, credit transfer opportunities, scholarships available and no GRE scores required, our online MEEM program is one of the best, most efficient and rewarding education opportunities around. 

Online Engineering Management Degree Highlights

  • Gain real world expertise in important engineering areas; courses include project management, human resources management, marketing, law and more.
  • Earn a graduate certificate in Six Sigma while enrolled in the master’s program (with a Graduate School application for the certificate program).
  • Prepare to receive the Black Belt Certification from the Institute of Industrial and Systems Engineers (IISE) with the Six Sigma certificate pathway.
  • Finish your degree in as little as 1 to 2 years.
  • Complete UofL's online stackable certificates as a pathway toward the master’s degree.
  • Learn from faculty with distinguished academic credentials, industry experience and a strong commitment to applied research.
  • Skip the test – no GRE or GMAT required for admission.

Is GRE Required for the Online MEEM Degree?

No. Unlike many graduate programs, the online MEEM at UofL does not require GRE test scores for admission. We believe that relevant work experience, community involvement, strong letters of recommendation and a clear professional goal statement carry more weight than standardized test performance alone.

By evaluating applicants based on their demonstrated profession passion, previous work experience and interpersonal skills, we aim to build a strong engineering management cohort poised to create meaningful change.
 

Transfer Prior College Credit to UofL’s Online MEEM 

For students who began their studies at another institution but wish to complete their online engineering management degree at UofL, we allow the transfer of up to 6 (six) credit hours or roughly two courses, from an accredited university, upon department approval.
 

Stack Graduate Certificates to Earn a Master’s Degree in Engineering

Several graduate certificates offered within the J.B. Speed School of Engineering can be stacked towards a MEEM without requiring additional coursework. Eligible certificate programs include, but are not limited to the following:

  • Analytics for Engineering Decision-Making (9 cr.hr.)
  • Engineering Logistics Certificate (9 cr.hr.)
  • Healthcare Systems Engineering Certificate (9 cr.hr.)
  • Six Sigma Certificate (9 cr.hr.)

Students must achieve a 3.0 or better cumulative grade point average to earn a certificate and only courses in which the student has earned a B or better may count toward the degree program.

Admissions Requirements for the Online MEEM

To be considered for admission, applicants must have:

  • A bachelor’s degree or its equivalent from an accredited institution
  • A minimum undergraduate GPA of 2.75 on a 4.0 scale (applicants who do not meet the minimum GPA may inquire about conditional acceptance)

*Required application packet materials include:

  • Transcripts showing a bachelor’s degree in science in engineering, math or related scientific field from an accredited institution
  • Completion of a college-level statistics course and Calculus I
  • A personal statement describing the applicant’s background and interest in engineering management or resume
  • Two letters of recommendation

*Your application portal:

Once you have started the graduate application, you can check the status and review any additional checklist to-do items. Log in to your application using the email address you used to apply for admission and your password. Your checklist items may include additional materials or documentation that facilitate a smooth admissions process. You will also have access to important contact information and next steps after an admissions decision is made.

What Else Should You Know?

The questions and answers below help you learn more about our program, courses, delivery or how to succeed as an online student at UofL and beyond. 

  • Is a master's in engineering management worth it?

Earning a master's in engineering management is a great choice for professionals who want a salary boost or are seeking career advancement opportunities. UofL’s program requires only 30 credit hours, offers credit transfer (6 credit hours from accredited universities), stackable certificates that can get you closer to your goal one level at the time and no GRE scores. This is one of the most efficient and valuable degrees out there.

  • What engineering roles or positions does the engineering management program prepare me for?

UofL's online master's degree in engineering management paves the way for a myriad of promising job outcomes. Graduates are well-positioned to assume leadership roles as project managers, orchestrating the successful execution of engineering projects from conception to delivery. They may also excel in roles as engineering consultants, providing expert guidance on project optimization and risk management. Additionally, opportunities abound in product management, operations management and quality assurance, where graduates leverage their unique blend of technical expertise and business acumen to drive innovation, efficiency and organizational success across various industries.

  • What bachelor’s degrees, other than engineering, are acceptable for admission in the engineering management master’s program?

UofL’s engineering management degree program requires a bachelor of science degree or its equivalent in engineering or related scientific field. Acceptable degrees may include physics, mathematics, chemistry, biochemistry and computer sciences. Bachelor's degrees in other fields, such as technology or social sciences degrees, are accepted so long as applicants have taken calculus I and a statistics course. We do accept students who may be lacking in these two areas. However, they may be conditionally admitted and required to complete the requirements as part of their program.

  • Is MEEM better than MBA?

It depends on what your goals are and what industry you are in. If your industry is predominantly focused around science and engineering, obtaining a master of engineering in engineering management over an MBA is probably the way to go. While an MBA covers broader business aspects, a MEEM program delves into the fusion of engineering and management, equipping you with expertise in areas such as corporate strategy, organizational design and technology integration. This specialized focus enhances your ability to lead in engineering-driven industries, setting you apart with a unique skill set that aligns precisely with technical innovation and leadership.

  • Can I receive credit for graduate courses completed at another institution?

Students may request department permission to transfer up to six (6) credit hours from another accredited graduate program.

  • Can military Tuition Assistance (TA) be used with this program? 

Yes, TA can be used with this program. Members of the U.S. Armed Forces, who provide the proper documentation, are eligible for the reduced military tuition rate of $250 per credit hour (subject to change without prior notice).
