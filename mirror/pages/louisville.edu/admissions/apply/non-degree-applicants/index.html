Non-degree students are students who wish to take undergraduate classes but not pursue an undergraduate degree. Non-degree students include:

Requirements:

  • The application for admissions
  • The application fee of $30

The University of Louisville admits students on a rolling basis, so once you submit your application, you can expect to hear about our admission decision within 5-7 business days. After completion, you may review your application in the New Cards Gateway.
