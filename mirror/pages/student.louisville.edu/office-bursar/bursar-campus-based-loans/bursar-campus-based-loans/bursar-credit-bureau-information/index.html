Credit Bureau Information

All campus-based student loans are reported to the Credit Bureau. Once your student loans go into repayment, you will be building either a positive or a negative credit history.

You may obtain a free credit report yearly. You can call, write, or go to the website to obtain a credit report. The web sites listed below offer other valuable financial information.

Equifax 
1-800-997-2493
Equifax website

Experian
1-800-397-3742
Experian Website

Transunion 
1-800-888-4213
Transunion Website

If you are a resident of Colorado, Georgia, Massachusetts, Maryland, New Jersey, or Vermont, your credit report is free.
