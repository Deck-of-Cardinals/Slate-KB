The University of Louisville (UofL) 
A Military Friendly® School 

UofL recognizes the unique challenges and circumstances faced by members of the armed forces and strives to provide the support and flexibility you need to succeed as a college student. 

No matter where you are in your military career — or wherever you are in the world* — we bring our classroom directly to you, helping you realize your potential and accomplish your goals.

Online programs at UofL are available across various industries and disciplines. Use the online program finder to determine the right fit for your military or civilian career goals.

Courses offered online are designated in the schedule of courses. To see online courses only, select "Online-Asynchronous" or "Online-Synchronous" in the mode of instruction field.

*except where OFAC restrictions apply

Application Fee Waiver

Veterans and all personnel currently serving in the Armed Forces of the United States (all branches and all components) benefit from waived application fees. Contact: Center for Military-Connected Students

Priority Registration

All personnel currently serving in the Armed Forces of the United States (all branches and all components) benefit from priority course registration. Contact: Center for Military-Connected Students

Benefits for Military-Connected Students

  1. 1
    UofL's Academic, Financial and Success Support

    To empower military-connected students to succeed academically and to transition from higher education to a successful, rewarding career, the University of Louisville provides the following benefits:

    • Reduced military tuition rate for eligible members enrolled in eligible online programs - see special tuition rates by program
    • Ability to return to the program after deployment assignments without penalty
    • Opportunity to earn college credit toward your degree for military experience and leadership training
    • Career guidance and planning services; transition assistance
    • A Center for Military-Connected Students team in service to you and supporting your goals

  2. 2
    Using Department of Veterans Affairs Education Benefits at UofL

    The process of using your education benefits can be overwhelming, but we’re here to help you every step of the way. Visit the Center for Military-Connected Students' Admissions for Military & Veterans page for more information. 

    You can also find out which education benefit is best for you at the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs Road Map for Success.

    For more information on all military benefits, contact milconstu@louisville.edu or 502.852.6442.
