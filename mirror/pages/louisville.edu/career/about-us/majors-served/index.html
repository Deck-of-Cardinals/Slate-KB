Students/Majors Served

The University Career Center serves 9 of the 12 colleges/schools, representing almost 16,000 undergraduate and graduate students in over 50 academic units.  Student appointments for career coaching are scheduled through Cardinal Careers. The following is a list of colleges and schools served by the UCC: 

1. College of Arts & Sciences 

  • Humanities: English, Fine Arts, Foreign Languages, Philosophy, Theatre
  • Natural Sciences: Biology, Chemistry, Mathematics, Neuroscience, Physics, Astronomy
  • Social Sciences: Anthropology, Communication, Criminal Justice, Geography, Geosciences, History, Pan-African Studies, Political Science, Psychology, Sociology, Urban/Public Affairs, Women's/Gender Studies

2. School of Dentistry 

  • Dentistry
  • Dental Hygiene

3. Education and Human Development 

  • Counseling
  • Exercise Science
  • Sports Administration
  • Human Resources and Organizational Development
  • Organizational Leadership and Learning
  • Teacher Education

4. Graduate School 

  •  Interdisciplinary Studies Major
  • Majors in colleges and schools served

5. Kent School of Social Work and Family Science


6. School of Medicine 

  • Integrated Programs in Biomedical Sciences
  • Speech-Language Pathology
  • NOT Medical Doctors or Post Docs

7. School of Music


8. School of Nursing


9. School of Public Health and Information Sciences

 

The University Career Center serves all exploratory students and posts all positions for Student Employment (part-time jobs) and Federal Work Study positions for all students.
