Change, Add or Declare a Major

What you need to know

Major changes can be pretty common. As you grow and progress during your college experience, you may find your strengths or interests lie in a different academic field than you first expected. 

You may also find that the coursework in your current school or college more difficult than you had anticipated. If you encounter academic difficulty in your current area, it may be possible to change your major and/or transfer to another school or college within the university.

    Know the requirements, make an informed decision

    When making decisions about your major, it's best to have all the information. Get to know the admissions and course requirements for your chosen major or minor. Requirements

College of Arts & SciencesA&S majors have varying admission requirements. Find them at: https://artsandsciences.louisville.edu/academics-programs/undergraduate. 
College of Business

BSBA degrees (Accountancy, Business Economics, Computer Information Systems, Economics, Equine Business, Finance, Global Supply Chain Management, Management, and Marketing)

  • 2.8 GPA and completion of MATH 111 or higher required for admission.

BBA degree

  • 2.5 GPA required for admission.
School of Dentistry (Dental Hygiene)
  • 2.8 combined UofL and transfer GPA required for admission to Lower Division.
  • 2.8 combined UofL and transfer GPA and 3.0 program GPA required to apply for application for selective Upper Division (3.5 GPA recommended, see info on their website).
College of Education & Human Development

Exercise Physiology and SPAD

  • 2.25 GPA required for admission.
  • 2.75 GPA and C or better in ENGL 102 and OC for professional portion of Exercise Physiology Physical & Health Education track

OLL

  • 2.0 UofL GPA (can petition if below)
  • Must have at least 1 year of supervised work experience

Teacher Education

  • 2.25 GPA required for admission.
  • 2.75 GPA required for admission to professional program.
Kent School of Social Work and Family Science
  • 2.0 GPA required for admission to Lower Division.
  • 2.5 GPA and 45 hours completed required for Upper Division. Upper Division admission not guaranteed with 2.5 GPA; other factors are also considered, see info here: https://louisville.edu/kent/admissions-1/bsw-admissions.
School of Medicine (Communication Sciences & Disorders)
  • Intended major admission
    • 3.0 college GPA (UofL and transfer work) and 12 hrs completed
  • Professional program admission
    • Completion of a minimum of 30 credit hours with a cumulative GPA of 3.0
    • Completion of CMDS 245 (Survey of Communication Disorders) with a grade of C or higher
    • Completion of CMDS 270 (Clinical Observation) with a grade of “pass”
    • Completion of an online program application.
School of Music

  • 2.75 GPA and successful audition required for admission (see info here: https://louisville.edu/music/apply/undergraduate/audition-requirements).

    (Music and New Media major requires a portfolio in place of an audition.)

School of Nursing
  • 2.8 college GPA (UofL and transfer work), 12 hrs completed at UofL to transfer into Lower Division, and no more than one grade of C- or below in any of the following same courses (includes transfer credit): MATH 111, BIOL 260, BIOL 261, BIOL 264, BIOL 265.
  • 2.8 combined UofL and transfer GPA, and 3.0 Program GPA required to apply for selective Upper Division (see info here: https://nursing.louisville.edu/admission/undergraduate-admissions/admissions-requirements-pre-licensure-bsn/admissions).
School of Public Health & Information Sciences
  • 2.0 GPA required for general BA and BS Public Health degrees.
  • Public Health BS with track in Professional Health Studies:
    • Completion of 200-level BIOL or CHEM with a grade of B or better.
    • Completion of MATH 109 or higher with a grade of B or better OR placement into a math course above the level of MATH 109.
    • 3.0 cumulative GPA.
Speed School of Engineering

BS Degrees (Bioengineering, Chemical Engineering, Civil Engineering, Computer Science and Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Industrial and Systems Engineering, and Mechanical Engineering)

  • 2.8 GPA and B- or better in MATH 190 or ENGR 100 and CHEM 101. 

Computer Science BA

  • Less than 12 hours earned: 3.0 high school GPA, ACT COMP and MATH 23+ or SAT MATH 560+; and must meet freshmen requirements and have 2.8 college GPA.
  • 12-23 hours earned: 2.8 GPA, ACT READ 20+ or completion of ENGL 101, and B- or better in MATH 111.
  • More than 24 hours earned: 2.8 GPA and B- or better in MATH 111.
Access the Form in ULink

You can change your major in ULink. Here's how:

  1. Access the “Change Major Request” by selecting the Academic Progress tile on the ULink Student Homepage. 
  2. Select “Change Major Request” from the sidebar on the left of your screen. The Change Major form will appear on screen.
  3. Identify when you would like the change to take effect in the “Effective Term” area (e.g., Fall 2025, Spring 2026).
  4. Designate if you are Currently Enrolled at UofL and if you would like to Double Major. 
  5. Click the appropriate button to add majors or minors. 
  6. A list of Academic Plans will appear on screen. There are three sections: Certificate, Exploratory Major and Major. To select an option, place a check in the checkbox next to your choice. (Note: Hovering over the question mark box will provide a short synopsis of the respective Academic Plan and clicking on the listed Academic Plan will open a Major Guide PDF file providing a full overview.)
  7. Once you have made your choice with the checkbox confirm your selection by clicking OK located at the top or bottom of the page. 
  8. Save your changes by clicking "Save and Continue."
  9. It will again ask you to verify the changes you have made. Once you have verified the information is correct, click OK. 
  10. Finally, you will receive a confirmation that the save was successful. Click OK.
  11. You will then see a confirmation page where you can: Track, Edit or Cancel your request. 
  12. You can visit the Next Steps link at the bottom of the page before leaving this screen. It will open a new window with additional admission information for your major.
