Student Athletic Fee Request

Beginning in 2025, the University of Louisville introduced a new student athletic fee and ticket model designed to align with ACC peers and enhance the student gameday experience.

The student fee goes directly to Louisville Athletics to support the operational expenses of all 23 sports, while also enabling stronger partnerships across campus. This model is built to strengthen student support, increase access and help deliver a championship-caliber experience – both for our students and our student-athletes.

Tickets are not first-come, first-served. You can request tickets at any point during the open window and timing will not impact your request priority.

If you did not meet the criteria to receive the fee, you can request to be included by filling out the form below.

*Please note, this form is only to Opt in into the Fee and not a request to Opt out.

Opt-In Eligibility

You must meet one of the criteria below:

  • Undergraduate students must be enrolled in at least 6 credit hours to be eligible.
  • Graduate students must be enrolled in at least 4.5 credit hours to be eligible.
  • Part-time students who meet the above thresholds may request to opt-in.
  • Students participating in Co-Op programs may opt in if they submit a request.
  • Faculty and Staff receiving tuition remission are not eligible to opt-in.

Additional Information

  • Please note, the form must be completed each Fall and Spring semester if you would like to opt in to the athletic fee.
  • Questions regarding eligibility or the process should be directed to the Office of the Bursar or the Athletic Ticket Office.
