Concourse Syllabi Management System

Concourse, a tool designed for centralized syllabus creation, management, and compliance.

Concourse Syllabus

 

Searching Concourse for Syllabi 

With Concourse, syllabi can be searched for by keywords, course titles, subject, prefix, course number, campus, school, department, instructor, session or term. This can also assist educators should they need to provide access or produce a report for program-level accreditation.

Concourse is available to UofL students, faculty, and staff at Concourse Syllabi Management System. Syllabus requests from alumni, community members, and other external users may be sent to the Office of the Registrar at regoff@louisville.edu. For system or functionality questions, please contact the OAPA Faculty and Staff Resources website.
