The Big Picture

The online Bachelor of Science in Psychology at the University of Louisville prepares students to understand the multi-faceted human behavior and apply psychological principles in professional and academic environments. This flexible, fully online program builds skills in research, analysis, communication and critical thinking for careers across industries or preparation for graduate study.

UofL’s nationally recognized bachelor’s in psychology explores psychological concepts, including brain-behavior relationships, abnormal psychology, cognition, mental health and human development. Our curriculum integrates these topics with essential research methods, providing a comprehensive foundation of psychological science.

Online BS in Psychology Degree Highlights

  • Learn about the factors influencing human behavior and how to apply psychological principles in various settings
  • Develop technological, research and statistical analysis skills to design solutions for societal, professional and individual challenges
  • Prepare for a career in counseling, HR or research, with versatile skills sought after by employers nationwide
  • Build upon your undergraduate degree and prepare for graduate studies in psychology or related fields
  • Earn a research-based degree taught by experienced faculty within the Department of Psychological and Brain Sciences, with extensive field authority
  • Transfer in your associate degree or up to 90 credit hours from other accredited institutions

Earning a bachelor’s in psychology online prepares you for various career opportunities by developing your interpersonal communication skills and knowledge. Whether you aspire to work in counseling, human resources or further academic research, the skills and insights gained from this degree can be pivotal in pursuing various professional paths.

What Are the Admissions Requirements for the Online Bachelor’s in Psychology?

New students and transfer Students with less than 24 transferable credits from accredited institutions must have:

  • High school diploma from an accredited institution (GED or equivalent also accepted)
  • Minimum high school GPA of 2.5 on a 4.0 scale; AND for transfer students, a minimum college GPA of 2.0 (applicants who do not meet the minimum GPA may inquire about conditional acceptance)
  • Composite ACT* score of 21 or higher (test optional admission is granted on a case-by-case basis)

Transfer students with 24 or more transferable credits from accredited institutions must have:

  • Minimum college GPA of 2.0 on a 4.0 scale (applicants who do not meet the minimum GPA may inquire about conditional acceptance)

*Your Application Portal:

Once you have started an undergraduate application, you can check the status and review any additional checklist to-do items. Log in to your application using the email address you used to apply for admission and your password. Your checklist items may include additional materials or documentation that facilitate a smooth admissions process. You will also have access to important contact information and next steps after an admissions decision is made.

No ACT/SAT? Test-optional admissions are granted on a case-by-case basis and require a comprehensive review of high school transcript, including grades and college-preparatory courses. A personal statement may be requested during the admissions review process.


Transfer Credit for Online BS in Psychology Students

Students may transfer up to 60 hours from a community college and up to 90 hours from an accredited 4-year college. Transfer applicants must have a minimum college GPA of 2.0 to be admitted in good standing. You can use the Transfer Evaluation System to get an idea of how many credits you'll be able to transfer in toward your UofL bachelor's degree, and visit our General Transfer Student Page [LD1] for more details.

  • Are you a KCTCS student? KCTCS students can transfer completed associate degrees in their entirety and begin immediately with online upper level coursework at UofL. Visit our KCTCS Transfers Page[LD2]  for more information.

See the UofL Transfer Credit Policy for more information.

What Will You Learn in the Online Bachelor’s in Psychology?

The online psychology curriculum at UofL is intentionally structured to move you from foundational principles to advanced application. You will begin with core concepts in behavior, cognition and development, then progress into research methods, statistical analysis and specialized topics such as psychopathology, brain and behavior and social processes. 

Electives allow you to tailor your studies to career interests, while the culminating experience strengthens your ability to analyze evidence, communicate findings and apply psychological science in professional settings.

Online Courses

The online bachelor’s in psychology from UofL is a 121 credit hour program. The program is 100% online and courses are typically delivered asynchronously in standard (15 weeks) term length, with some courses also available in the summer and winter terms.  See below curriculum structure and components:

  1. General Education (Cardinal Core) Requirements: 31 credit hours
  2. Arts & Sciences Requirements: 13-15 credit hours
  3. Psychology Required Core Courses: 42 credit hours
  4. Supporting Courses: 12 credit hours
  5. Electives: 21-23 credit hours

To see all available online courses and degree requirements, please visit UofL's Academic Catalog page (select Degree Requirements tab).

What Can I Do With a Bachelor’s in Psychology?

Earning a psychology degree prepares you for a wide variety of career opportunities. According to the National Association of Colleges and Employers, hiring managers seek employees with leadership, teamwork, communication and problem-solving abilities, above all others. 

This program equips you with robust critical thinking and research skills, while developing your understanding of foundational psychological concepts, to help you succeed and stand out in the career, passion or path of your choice.

According to Lightcast.io Occupation Outlook data, on average, jobs in psychology related fields are projected to grow 8% between 2025 and 2033, with 166,584 annual job postings. The median salary for those jobs is nearly $67,800. Career opportunities and earnings vary based on actual job role, geographic location, prior experience and employer. These figures reflect overall industry trends and are not a guarantee of individual results. 

Many bachelor’s in psychology graduates pursue careers as a substance abuse, behavioral disorder or mental health counselor. This degree can be a stepping stone for pursuing a career as a psychologist. Many graduates also pursue careers in the following fields:

  • Market research
  • Public relations
  • Family advocacy and support

What Else Should You Know?

The questions and answers below help you learn more about our program, delivery method, application and admission process, financial aid options and how to succeed as an online student at UofL and beyond.

  • Can I be a therapist with this degree?

While a BS in Psychology is a valuable and foundational degree, it's important to note that becoming a licensed therapist typically requires additional education and training. Most therapists hold at least a master's degree in psychology or a related field and must complete supervised clinical hours to obtain licensure. Our program can be a great stepping stone towards furthering your education if your goal is to become a licensed therapist.

  • Is this program fully asynchronous?

Yes, the online BS in Psychology program is designed to be fully asynchronous. This means that you can access course materials, participate in discussions and complete assignments at a time that suits your schedule. Asynchronous learning offers greater flexibility for students who may have work, family or other commitments.

  • How many weeks are in one term for each class?

Each class in the online BS in Psychology program typically runs for a duration of 15 weeks, the standard term length for a bachelor’s degree.

  • How much time per week will I spend on this program?

The amount of time you will need to dedicate to the program each week can vary depending on the individual and the specific courses you are taking. As a general guideline, you should expect to spend approximately 2-3 hours per credit hour weekly for your course(s). This includes time for reading, assignments, discussion participation and studying. Keep in mind that time management and study habits play a significant role in your success in the program.

  • Is financial aid available for this program?

Yes, there is financial aid available for this program. You can learn more by visiting our Financial Aid tab on the Tuition page.
