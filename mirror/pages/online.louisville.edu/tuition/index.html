Tuition & Financial Aid for Online Programs

At the University of Louisville, pursuing your degree online goes beyond flexibility and convenience – it’s a powerful step toward your future. 

Our programs are built for growing professionals, career shifters and lifelong learners ready to make an impact. We aim to provide you with clear tuition information, so you have the insight, tools and confidence to plan your path and focus on what matters most – achieving your goals.

  • At UofL, tuition is the same for in-state and out-of-state students enrolled in a fully online program. 
    Note: students enrolled in non-online plan codes (e.g., taking a single online course, etc.) will be charged a tuition rate based on residency.
     
  • The tuition rate does not include costs associated with a specific course or program, such as textbooks. Tuition and fees are subject to change at any time without prior notice. The summary on this page is intended to provide a quick reference for students. 
     

For more university-wide information and updates, visit the UofL Bursar's page.
