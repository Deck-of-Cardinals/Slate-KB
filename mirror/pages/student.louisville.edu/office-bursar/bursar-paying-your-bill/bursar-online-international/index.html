Preferred Payment Method

The preferred payment method for international students is PayMyTuition via ULink.

University of Louisville has partnered with PayMyTuition for international tuition payments. With PayMyTuition, you can pay your tuition payments from any bank, in any country in any currency at better than bank exchange rates. PayMyTuition is fast, simple and cost effective.

How to Make a Payment

  1. 1
    Sign in to ULink

    Sign in to your ULink student portal.  Select the “Student” tile, from the ULink log in options.

  2. 2
    Click the “Financial Account” tile
  3. 3
    Select the “International Payments” tab

    From the left-navigation panel, select the “International Payments” tab.

  4. 4
    Click the “International Tuition Payment"

    Click the “International Tuition Payment” button to be redirected to the payment portal.

NOTE: **Itemized charges are not shown in PayMyTuition. In ULink, click the “Financial Account” tile then select “Transaction Summary” to review any individual charges.**

PayMyTuition customer support information

Call 1-855-663-6839 (toll-free) or through one of their local country contact numbers. You can also reach PayMyTuition Support at support@paymytuition.com or through their support page.
