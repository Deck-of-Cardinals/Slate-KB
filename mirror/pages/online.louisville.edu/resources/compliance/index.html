Important information for online students

UofL Online is committed to delivering high-quality online education that is compliant with federal and state regulations. It is important to understand that authorization and approval requirements may vary by state and program and our online programs may not be available in certain locations, including those outside the United States.

The information below outlines key compliance considerations so you understand how these requirements may apply to you and your program of interest.
