Entitlements and Deferments

Federal Perkins Student Loans offer many entitlements and deferments. While some entitlements are dependent on what year the borrower signed the promissory note, several entitlements are universal to all Perkins loan borrowers including Student Deferment, Economic Hardship Deferment, Unemployment Deferment, and Forbearance.

If you feel that you may qualify for one of these entitlements, please print the appropriate form and mail the completed document to the University of Louisville, Bursar's Office-Loans, Louisville, KY 40292.

For any questions concerning your Perkins loan, please call (502) 852-6503 to speak to a loan repayment specialist.

Visit the ECSI website for information about Economic Hardship, Unemployment, Forbearance, and Special Payment Arrangement Request as well as Student Deferment, Fellowship, Internship/Residency, and Military Deferment.
