Student Recreation Center Fee

Beginning fall of 2011 semester, a $98 per semester recreation fee will be applied to all new students (including graduate, professional and readmitted students) and pro-rated, per credit hour, for less than full-time students.

Contact the Intramural Sports Office by email , or phone 502-852-6707, if you have any questions.
