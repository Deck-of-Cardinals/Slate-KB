Scholarships for Online Students

The University of Louisville offers need-based and merit-based scholarships to help enrolled online students alleviate the cost of their college education.

To demonstrate need, students are required to complete the Free Application for Federal Student Aid (FAFSA). Students may qualify for more than one scholarship and are encouraged to explore all possible avenues for funding, from and outside of UofL. 

There are numerous scholarship opportunities and application deadlines vary. To obtain a scholarship that fits your needs and goals, you should explore all options and apply as much in advance as possible, ahead of the following academic year start. 

Scholarship Options for UofL Online Enrolled Students

New undergraduate students should visit the websites below to learn more about scholarships awarded upon admission or through the Student Financial Aid Office’s (SFAO) scholarship application process.

Office of Transfer and Adult Services

Online program students are eligible for the following transfer scholarships:

  • KCTCS Academic Transfer Scholarship
  • Guaranteed Transfer Scholarship
     

Student Financial Aid Office (SFAO) Scholarships

Students pursuing an online degree at the University of Louisville may be eligible for scholarships managed by the SFAO. All scholarships through SFAO are managed via one application. The application deadline is February 15 for the upcoming academic year.

Awards available for online non-traditional students include:

  • Continuing Undergraduate Student Academic Scholarship
  • Beth K. Fields Scholarship/Fields Alumni Scholarship
  • New Beginning Scholarship Fund
  • Zonta Club of Louisville Inez Webb Award
  • Cardinal Commitment Grant - Transfer Students
  • Cardinal Commitment Grant - First Time in College

UofL academic departments also provide scholarship awards for both the undergraduate and graduate levels, with varying application processes and timelines. Learn more about awards that may be available for the noted online programs by visiting the page(s) linked below.

  • Arts and Sciences
  • College of Business
  • College of Education and Human Development
  • Kent School of Social Work
  • Psychological and Brain Sciences Scholarships
  • School of Nursing

  • WGS Studies Scholarships 

Students can use scholarships offered by external organizations to help with educational expenses. We continue to update our list of potential funding sources for online transfer and/or graduate students, so please check back often. These scholarships are not affiliated with UofL.
