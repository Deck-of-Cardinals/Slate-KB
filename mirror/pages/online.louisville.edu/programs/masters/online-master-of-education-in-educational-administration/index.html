Master of Education in Educational Administration, Online

The online Master of Education in Educational Administration (MEdEA) is a fully synchronous online graduate degree designed to prepare seasoned educators to become K-12 principals and administrators. Through in-depth coursework and a 10-week in-person practicum with a principal mentor, graduate students learn about what it takes to successfully lead in education settings.

The educational administration master’s program at UofL prepares graduates in Kentucky and elsewhere to advance educational goals at a site-wide level as school principals and administrators. You will learn leadership skills that can drive success at the school and district levels and beyond. As a K-12 school principal you can improve the educational outcomes of students outside of the classroom and build a learning community that empowers educators and students and their families.

This program focuses on transformational and instructional leadership principles. Additionally, you will gain practical knowledge on pertinent topics on policy, K-12 educational law, human resources and fiscal management. Graduates of the program are equipped to meet the unique challenges in educational administration.

The MEd in Educational Administration online degree is offered by the College of Education and Human Development at the University of Louisville, a school consistently recognized by US News & World Report as Best in the Nation and Best for Veterans for its Graduate Education Programs’ quality of instruction and student support.

The Big Picture

School principals are the heartbeat of thriving schools. They lead educators to strive for excellence, encourage students and build partnerships with families and their community. The online MEd in Educational Administration is designed for seasoned teachers with at least 3 years of K-12 teaching experience who are ready to advance outside the classroom and become leaders at the site administration level. This program combines education in leadership fundamentals with applied practice to prepare students for the realities of school administration. 

Synchronous Learning

Delivered online in a synchronous format, UofL’s master’s degree in education administration offers flexibility for working educators while maintaining rigorous academic expectations aligned with professional standards for school principals.

Through a structured cohort format, synchronous curriculum and a 10-week in-person Summer practicum with an active school principal mentor, students develop the knowledge, skills and professional judgement required to support and build successful learning communities.

Online MEdEA Degree Highlights

  • Develop the skills and practical experience to lead thriving learning communities
  • Prepare for professional practice and licensure as a school principal
  • Learn from experienced faculty and a currently serving school principal mentor
  • Translate your skills and experience into higher pay and more influence within your school and district

This program fulfills the educational requirements for Level 1 or Level 2 Principal Certification in Kentucky.

What Are the Admissions Requirements for the Online MEd in Educational Administration?

To be considered for admission, applicants must have:

  • Three years of successful K-12 teaching experience in Kentucky or three years (one year = a minimum of 140 contract days) of successful K-12 teaching experience outside the state of Kentucky
  • a bachelor’s degree or its equivalent from an accredited institution
  • a minimum undergraduate GPA of 3.0 in undergraduate work on a 4.0 scale

*Required application packet materials include:

  • valid Kentucky Teaching Certificate or statement of eligibility for candidates outside Kentucky
  • submission of an online Graduate School application.
  • letter of intent for pursuing the degree which references your leadership intentions
  • current resume
  • philosophy of education (2 page limit)
  • letter of Support from current school principal. 
  • signed statement regarding the Acceptable Use of Technology Agreement and Professional Code of Ethics for Kentucky School Personnel. 
  • joint screening by the Department of Educational Leadership, Evaluation and Organizational Development and district personnel.

What Will You Learn in the Online Master of Education in Educational Administration?

The online master’s in education is a 30 credit hour synchronous program. Students must complete nine 8 week synchronous courses and one synchronous course that runs the entire semester. Over the summer semester students must complete a 10 week professional internship with a local school principal that spans the end of the school year, summer preparation and the next school year start to gain valuable operational and instructional knowledge and skills.  

Most of the online synchronous courses meet on Wednesday evenings and cover topics including many aspects of leadership in educational settings, methodology for curriculum and student performance oversite along with overall school improvement within Kentucky’s required diagnostic reviews for underperforming schools. Through coursework and hands-on experience you will learn how to lead, transform and empower flourishing learning communities.

Online Courses

Courses are synchronous, delivered in 8 week and standard term length. Please note that the synchronous delivery will require specific class time commitments.

What Else Should You Know

The questions and answers below help you learn more about our program, delivery method, application and admission process, financial aid options and how to succeed as an online student at UofL and beyond. 

  • Are classes available to complete the master's degree entirely online?

Yes! You can complete the program coursework 100% online, but all online coursework is completed via synchronous sessions (from 4:30 to 7 p.m. ET on specific weekdays). There is also an in-person internship requirement and course. You will be assigned to a mentor principal in a school geographically close to you unless you are located in a state where restrictions apply. If you are considering completing the internship outside of Kentucky, please contact the Director of Educator Preparation Student Services at betty.hampton@louisville.edu before enrolling in the program.

  • May I transfer credits from another institution or program into my master's program?

Yes, you may request to transfer up to 6 credit hours from an accredited institution you attended previously, once you are admitted to the program. The credit transferred must be approved by your UofL advisor. Transfer requests are then submitted through the Graduate School at UofL.

  • How long does it take to complete this program?

The Educational Administration program is designed to be completed over 5 consecutive semesters, or 18 months as part of a consistent cohort of fellow students.

  • How much does the online Master of Education in Educational Administration from UofL cost?

The standard graduate tuition rate of $863 per credit hour. The University of Louisville's base tuition rate is determined annually once the Kentucky State Legislature approves the recommendations from the University and the Council on Postsecondary Education (CPE). The University of Louisville offers $250 per credit hour military tuition rate for this program. Review our military rates page for more information. 

  • What should I write about in my letter of intent and philosophy of education admission materials?

The letter of intent should present why you want to pursue this degree and what your professional plans are once you have completed the degree. Your philosophy of education should describe how you envision the role of education in the lives of students, educators and the communities they live and work in.

  • What is the in-person professional internship?

A key component of the MEd in Educational Administration is the in-person professional internship with an active school principal. Students in the program dedicate one summer term to a 10 week, 3 credit hour course (LEAD 627 Internship in Educational Leadership: Principal). Students are paired with a local principal for the end of the school year, the summer planning period and the start of the next school year to gain hands-on experience and mentorship from that principal. Students will sign a contract for their internship highlighting a project to be completed during the internship that is aimed at improving outcomes for students and/or adults. If you are considering completing the internship outside of Kentucky, please contact the Director of Educator Preparation Student Services at betty.hampton@louisville.edu before enrolling in the program. 

  • Will this program lead to principal certification?

Yes, this program prepares students to meet the educational requirements for Level 1 or Level 2 Principal Certification in Kentucky. If you plan to pursue professional licensure or certification in another state you should review the licensure determinations and disclosures dashboard and information on the online learning compliance pages prior to enrollment. We recommend that you also contact your state’s licensing board directly to verify that the requirements have not changed recently and to answer any questions especially those regarding additional requirements beyond the degree. 

  • What can I do with the MEd in Educational Administration degree?

Completing the MEdEA degree academically prepares students for licensure eligibility as an administrator or principal and meets the curricular requirements for endorsement. If you don’t pursue licensure as a K-12 school principal you can use the leadership and policy education from the program to excel in roles including district level administrator, policy analyst, curriculum developer, or move into higher education administration.

Demand for well-prepared educational administrators is at an all-time high. According to Lightcast.io occupation outlook data, on average, jobs in the education field are projected to grow 4% between 2025 and 2033 with 237,945 annual job postings. The median salary for school principals is nearly $98,700 (Lightcast.io). Career opportunities and earnings vary based on actual job role, geographic location, prior experience and employer. These figures reflect overall industry trends and are not a guarantee of individual results. 

  • Do I earn the same degree that on-campus students earn? Does the diploma say “online”?

Your degree – just like the quality of your courses – will be the same as those received by on campus students. It will not contain the method of course delivery (i.e. “online”).
