Directions to the Belknap Campus

Exits for the University of Louisville are directly off I-65 North and South

  1. If coming from I-265 (Gene Snyder) or I-264 (Watterson Expressway), proceed North on I-65 to exit 133 University Blvd, turning left at the light which brings you to Belknap Campus. Alternatively, you can take exit 134 Arthur Street which brings you on the North side of campus.
  2. If coming from I-71 South or I-64 East or West, proceed south on I-65 to exit 133 University Blvd, turning left at the light which brings you to Belknap Campus.

Directions to the Office of the Bursar

(located in Houchens building)

From South of Louisville: Exit 133 - University Blvd.

  1. Take I-65 North (Toward Indianapolis) to Exit 133 - University Blvd.
  2. Turn left at the stoplight onto University Blvd.
  3. Continue through the next stoplight (Speedway gas station on right).
  4. Turn right onto Floyd St (McDonald's on corner).
  5. Continue through the first light at the East entrance of the parking garage (this entrance is permit only).
  6. At the second light, turn left into the Student Activities Center (SAC).
  7. On your left, you will see the visitor parking entrance to the garage.
  8. After parking, take the stairs or elevator to the third floor of the garage.
  9. Take the pedestrian bridge (center of the garage) over the railroad tracks, take the stairs or elevator down to ground level.
  10. The Houchens Building (gray brick) will be on your left at the bottom of the stairs.
  11. Enter the Houchens Building from the front and enter the Bursar's Office (Room 101) on the left.

From North of Louisville: Exit 133 – University Blvd.

  1. Take I-65 South (Toward Nashville) to Exit 133 – University Blvd.
  2. Turn left at the stoplight onto University Blvd.
  3. Continue through the next stoplight (Speedway gas station on right).
  4. Turn right onto Floyd St (McDonald's on corner).
  5. Continue through the first light at the East entrance of the parking garage (this entrance is permit only).
  6. At the second light, turn left into the Student Activities Center (SAC).
  7. On your left, you will see the visitor parking entrance to the garage.
  8. After parking, take the stairs or elevator to the third floor of the garage.
  9. Take the pedestrian bridge (center of the garage) over the railroad tracks, take the stairs or elevator down to ground level.
  10. The Houchens Building (gray brick) will be on your left at the bottom of the stairs.
  11. Enter the Houchens Building from the front and enter the Bursar's Office (Room 101) on the left.

From North of Louisville (alternative option): Exit 134 – Arthur Street

  1. Take I-65 South (Toward Nashville) to Exit 134 – Arthur Street.
  2. Merge onto Arthur Street.
  3. Turn right at the stop sign onto Brandeis Ave/Cardinal Blvd.
  4. At the roundabout, take the third exit onto Floyd St.
  5. At the first stoplight, turn right into the Student Activities Center (SAC).
  6. On your left, you will see the visitor parking entrance to the garage.
  7. After parking, take the stairs or elevator to the third floor of the garage.
  8. Take the pedestrian bridge (center of the garage) over the railroad tracks, take the stairs or elevator down to ground level.
  9. The Houchens Building (gray brick) will be on your left at the bottom of the stairs.
  10. Enter the Houchens Building from the front and enter the Bursar's Office (Room 101) on the left.
