Online Student Advising

Going back to school or continuing your education online may seem like a complex process with a lot of moving parts, but you don’t have to figure it all out on your own. Our online academic advisors are here to help you build a clear plan, explore your options and stay on track from your first course to your final one.

You bring your goals, your life experience and your questions. We’ll provide the guidance, resources and answers you need to keep moving forward. Whether you’re mapping out your next term, dealing with a challenge or celebrating a win, your advisor is your go-to resource for navigating your coursework through graduation. 

If you are a transfer student, contact your program's advising unit to set up an appointment via phone or e-mail before you begin classes. Of course, you are welcome to come to campus for advising if you live nearby. 

If you are not a transfer student, refer to your letter of admission to determine whether you need to be advised.
