Master of Science in Sport Administration, 100% Online

The Big Picture

The sport industry does not reward passion alone. It rewards strategic leaders who understand finance, governance, analytics and communication at an advanced level. The online Master of Science in Sport Administration at the University of Louisville is designed for professionals ready to expand their influence, strengthen decision-making authority and compete at the highest levels of sport business.

The MS in Sport Administration online degree can help you prepare for leadership roles and develop or sharpen the tools you may already have while equipping you to manage complex and dynamic projects and challenges in various sport environments or organizations. 

Whether you work in collegiate athletics, professional sport, recreation or sport marketing, this degree can give you the knowledge needed to expand your playbook and move from operational contributor to organizational decision-maker. 

Online MS in Sport Administration Degree Highlights

  • Enrich your leadership skills and effectiveness in sport business including management, research, communication, marketing, policy and law
  • Learn from guest lecturers actively leading sport business organizations
  • Advance expertise in sport finance, analytics, leadership and policy
  • Strengthen strategic decision-making for complex sport organizations
  • Earn your master’s degree in as little as 1 year

As sport organizations grow more complex and revenue-driven, leadership roles increasingly demand professionals with graduate-level expertise in analytics, finance and organizational strategy. Enterprises of all sizes seek professionals who can manage budgets, interpret data, navigate policy and guide teams through change.

What Admissions Requirements Are for the Online MSSPAD

To be considered for admission, applicants must have:

  • a bachelor’s degree or its equivalent from an accredited institution
  • a minimum undergraduate GPA of 2.75 on a 4.0 scale (applicants who do not meet the minimum GPA may inquire about conditional acceptance)
  • applicants may need to speak to or demonstrate prior course work and experience

*Required application packet materials include:

  • a personal statement (500 words or less) explaining your interest in the program and long-term objectives
  • two letters of recommendation
  • current resume
  • virtual interview with the program

Please note, faculty may require interviews as part of the admission process.

What Will You Learn in the Online Master of Science in Sport Administration?

The master’s in sport administration online curriculum at UofL is structured to build executive-level capability across the business dimensions of sport. Core coursework includes advanced sport finance and economics, sport analytics, strategic marketing, sport policy, sport communication and legal issues in sport organizations. You will also complete research-focused study that aims to strengthen analytical thinking and evidence-based decision-making.

An applied internship experience allows you to integrate theory with professional practice, reinforcing your ability to lead within real-world sport environments. By graduation, you will possess the financial literacy, legal awareness and strategic perspective required to manage modern sport enterprises.

Online Courses

The online Master of Science in Sport Administration is a 33 credit hour program that requires 27 credit hours in core courses and 6 credit hours in electives (as approved by advisor). The program is 100% online. Courses are asynchronous, delivered in standard term length.

To see all available online courses and degree requirements, please visit UofL's Academic Catalog page (select Degree Requirements tab).

What Can I Do with a Master's in Sport Administration?

The expertise you’ll develop as a student in the Sport Administration master’s program enables you to become an effective leader and decision maker in sports business. You’ll develop a deeper understanding of management, finance, leadership, ethics, communication and marketing, as well as policy and law practiced within the field.

Some of the sport administration skills you will acquire include:

  • Ability to apply financial and managerial accounting concepts that emphasize understanding annual reports, financial analysis ratios as well as methods for increasing revenue and cost control
  • Ability to examine legal issues relevant to the field such as negligence, constitutional law, employment law, as well as intellectual property law and contract law
  • Ability to plan and conduct health and sport sciences research for organizational growth, workforce development and talent management.

What Can I Do With an Online MSSPAD?

Graduates of this program are qualified to pursue a variety of sports administration, management and leadership jobs, as they are prepared to take the next step in their sport admin careers. Some of the positions and fields where you can find our graduates include:

  • Facility & operations director
  • Event management executive
  • Sport marketing director
  • Sport analytics manager

What Else Should You Know?

The questions and answers below help you learn more about our program, delivery method, application and admission process and how to succeed as an online student at UofL and beyond.

  • Is a master’s in sport administration worth it?

For professionals seeking advancement into leadership or executive-level roles, this degree provides advanced expertise in finance, analytics, policy and organizational leadership. Higher level roles and positions across various organizations demand a higher level of preparation, knowledge and experience. This degree helps bring all skills together.

  • Is the program accredited?

The MS program is fully accredited through the Commission on Sport Management Accreditation (COSMA). Our program was the first in the country accredited at all three levels (undergraduate, master’s and doctoral).

  • How are applications evaluated?

The minimum guidelines for unconditional admission are an undergraduate GPA of 2.75. However, we review the entire application holistically and strongly consider the applicant's goals, recommendations, and related work experiences in addition to academic credentials.

  • Do I need any specific undergraduate major to be admitted? Are there any prerequisites?

We admit applicants from all undergraduate majors, with a few having undergraduate degrees in sport administration or sport management. You are not required to take any prerequisite courses before beginning the program.

  • What classes are required?

The completion of the program requires 33 credit hours (including 3 hours of internship) where 27 credit hours represent core courses (these 9 courses are required for all students).

The remaining 6 credit hours represent the elective courses. We offer multiple electives each semester across multiple interest areas. In addition, classes in other departments may be taken as electives if approved by your academic advisor. This flexibility allows you to customize the program to meet your needs.

  • When are the classes scheduled?

All required courses are delivered online and offered during either fall or spring semesters. Additional courses may be offered during summer sessions.

  • Can I transfer credits?

Yes. You can transfer up to 6 graduate credits from an accredited university. See the Graduate School Transfer of Credit Policy for more information.

  • Is there an exit requirement for the program?

To complete the program, students must have a minimum 3.0 GPA. No credit shall be accepted for carrying a grade lower than C (no C-minus).

  • How long is the program?

Most students can complete the program in 1.5 to 2 years. The length of the program typically depends on three factors:

  • Academic pace – the number of classes taken. For an accelerated pace, you can take up to 12 hours per semester.
  • Work schedule – whether you volunteer or work in the industry during the program.
  • Internship choice – whether you complete the required internship while taking classes, during the summer or at the end of the program.
