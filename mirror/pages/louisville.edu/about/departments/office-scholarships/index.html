How to Use Scholarship Universe

    Log in to Scholarship Universe about
  2. 2
    View your Dashboard

    Your view of current and future scholarship opportunities and any action items needing your attention.

  3. 3
    Answer Matching Questions

Scholarship Universe FAQ

Scholarship Universe

Scholarship Universe is a platform that maximizes scholarship opportunities for students by matching them to eligible scholarships. Scholarship Universe allows students to create an academic profile and add additional information about themselves to increase the opportunity to be matched to internal and external scholarship opportunities based on eligibility criteria.

Internal scholarships refer to scholarships directly awarded by University of Louisville, and external scholarships refer to any scholarships awarded by an outside source. Every external scholarship posted in Scholarship Universe has been vetted to ensure it came from a reliable source.

Scholarship Universe manages the gifted and endowed scholarships provided to the University from the generous support of community donors. There are some scholarships funded by the University that are managed by individual departments.

  • Students answer questions that match them to scholarship opportunities that they are eligible for.
  • Students easily apply online to multiple scholarship opportunities through a personalized portal. 
  • Students track their application status online via the portal.
  • Students are alerted whenever they are matched to a new scholarship opportunity.
  • Students receive automated reminders about pending tasks and the next steps.
