Master of Science in Computer Science, Online

The online master of science in computer science (MSCS) is designed for professionals looking to grow their computer science expertise, excel and advance fast in a field that does not stand still. UofL’s online program delivers critical knowledge across key areas in today’s environment including database design and management, artificial intelligence, analytics, data mining and much more. 

Designed for working professionals and career switchers with a technical foundation, this program can strengthen your command of core computer science concepts and new or emerging technologies while growing your expertise in high-demand areas shaping today’s digital economy.

You’ll graduate prepared to solve complex problems, adapt to change and lead innovation across organizations and industries where advanced computing skills drive growth and opportunity.

The Master of Science in Computer Science is offered by the J.B. Speed School of Engineering which has been consistently recognized as Best in the Nation and Best for Veterans by U.S. News & World Report for its Graduate Engineering degree programs, academic quality and excellent student support.

What Are Admissions Requirements for the Online MSCS?

To be considered for admission, applicants must have:

  • a bachelor’s degree or its equivalent from an accredited institution
  • a minimum undergraduate GPA of 2.75 on a 4.0 scale (applicants who do not meet the minimum GPA may inquire about conditional acceptance)

Required Application Packet Materials Include:

  • a one-page personal statement describing the applicant’s background and interest in computer science or a professional resume
  • two letters of recommendation

*Your application portal:

Once you have started the graduate application, you can check the status and review any additional checklist to-do items. Log in to your application using the email address you used to apply for admission and your password. Your checklist items may include additional materials or documentation that facilitate a smooth admissions process. You will also have access to important contact information and next steps after an admissions decision is made.

International Students

If you live outside of the United States and intend to complete an online academic program from your home country, be sure to view special requirements for international student. Online students do not qualify for US visa.

International students whose primary language is not English must show English language proficiency by either TOEFL/IELTS/Duolingo score or demonstration of a degree awarded from an acceptable English language institution. The successful applicant will typically have a TOEFL score of 79 or higher or overall lELTS score of 6.5 or higher or a Duolingo score of 105 or higher.

Online MSCS Transfer Credits

You may transfer up to six credit hours of graduate coursework from an accredited university, pending department approval. See the Graduate School Transfer of Credit Policy for more information.

What You Learn in the Online MSCS

Coursework in the online MSCS builds advanced knowledge across core and emerging computer science disciplines, including algorithms, software engineering, databases, artificial intelligence, data mining, analytics, computer systems and distributed systems.

Through a balance of theoretical depth and applied learning, students strengthen problem-solving, system design and analytical skills while gaining experience with modern computing architectures, data-driven decision-making and scalable software solutions. 

Electives and project or thesis options allow you to tailor the curriculum to your career goals while maintaining a strong technical foundation aligned with industry demand.

Online Courses

The online MSCS is a 30-credit hour program, with each course delivered in a 15-week course format (Fall/Spring) or 10-week (Summer), 100% online.

To see all available online courses and degree requirements, please visit UofL's Academic Catalog page (select Degree Requirements tab). 
View Course Requirements 

The program requires minimum 15 credit hours of 600 level courses. Electives must be chosen so that at least one-half of the credits counted toward the degree are at 600-level.

What Else Should I Know?

The questions and answers below help you learn more about our program and how to succeed as an online student at UofL and beyond.

  • Is the GRE/GMAT required?

No, this program does not require the GRE/GMAT tests. Rather than focusing on test scores, we place greater emphasis on your overall academic preparation, technical background and professional experience to assess your potential for success.

  • Can I do the thesis option as an online student?

Yes! Online students are welcome to do the thesis, project or course-only option.

  • Are there career services for this program?

Yes! The J.B. Speed School of Engineering offers career services for alumni and current students, including online students. For more information, please visit this webpage.

  • My degree is not in computer science or a related area. Can I still apply?

Yes, the pre-requisites are designed as if students do not have any knowledge in Computer Science and completing the 3 pre-requisite classes will serve as a foundation for a student to be successful in this program. The pre-requisite classes include: CSE 130, Intro to C & C++ Programming, CSE 310 Discrete Structure, and CSE 503, Survey of CSE: Data Structure & Design of Operating Systems.
