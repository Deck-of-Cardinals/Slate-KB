UofL Email

Prior to accessing your UofL email, you will need to activate your account in our Identity Management System.

Outlook is the UofL's preferred email system for employees and students. With UofL’s Office365, you can either use a web-based version or their downloadable apps. See Microsoft’s training for more information. You can download Office365 products on up to 5 devices.

Everyone at UofL gets two email addresses – userID@louisville.edu is the main email (also called system email address), and firstname.lastname@louisville.edu is an alias of the main account (also called the preferred nickname address). Email at either address end up in the same system account. You can change your preferred nickname address if it is available.

Follow these steps to change your preferred email address: ULink Outside > Resources > Email Addresses > Sign-in Student System> Chosen Nickname > Edit > Save

Updates in the following systems or locations:
Student System, WorkdayHR, O365 (Email Directory), University Directory (People Finder), Archibus, Library, Campus Health, Cardinal Card, Blackboard, Identity Management, Campus Solutions, Adobe

  • Preferred email is prioritized for use within most systems unless userID email is required for sign-in.

Students, upon graduation and once our Student System recognizes you as a UofL alum, are eligible to set up permanent email forwarding. Emails sent to your university account will be forwarded to the personal email account of your choosing. To set up email forwarding, go to our Identity Management system under My Profile > My Availability > Email for Life > switch option to Yes > Enter forwarding email address > re-enter forwarding email address > Save. If you do not see the option as listed, please be patient for matriculation data to be acknowledged.

Phones and Webex

Webex is the University of Louisville's telephone service. The Webex application can be used on a desktop, laptop, tablet or cellular device to make and recieve phone calls using your UofL phone number.

If an existing number and/or desk set is available through your department or unit, or if you need a new number or phone, contact our team.

If an existing phone number is connected to a desk set through your department or unit, or if you have a jammer or Webex phone service, contact our team.

E911 service allows you to make emergency calls using your VoIP or Webex phone service. Emergency Enhanced 911 (E911) is for digital devices not within a fixed location. For E911, the caller's number (set by ITS) and registered address information (set by the user) are automatically provided to the emergency operator. You must keep the address of your device updated each time you change your location.

For issues with Webex phone service, contact our team or HelpDesk for assistance.
