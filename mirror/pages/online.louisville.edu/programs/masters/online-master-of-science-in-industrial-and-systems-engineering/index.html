Master of Science in Industrial and Systems Engineering, Online

The graduate degree at the University of Louisville is designed for working professionals across a variety of industry backgrounds looking to expand their technical engineering skills, as well as business concepts understanding, who are preparing to lead diverse teams and projects or solve complex engineering problems.

Industrial and systems engineers are problem-solvers at scale. They design smarter systems, reduce inefficiencies and turn complex data into strategic decisions.

UofL’s online MS in industrial and systems engineering offers a broad range of courses across three focus areas in high demand across industries, with strong market growth potential and an engineering lens applicable to:

  • Human factors
  • Data analytics and operations research
  • Logistics and supply chain

The online master of science in industrial and systems engineering is offered through the J.B. Speed School of Engineering at the University of Louisville. UofL’s School of Engineering has been consistently recognized by U.S. News & World Report among Best in the Nation for its Graduate Engineering programs, academic excellence and student support.

The Big Picture

The online MSISE equips you with advanced analytical tools and cross-functional expertise to improve performance across manufacturing, health care, logistics, technology and beyond.

This 30-credit-hour graduate degree program is uniquely positioned to help you succeed where engineering principles and systems thinking intersect. The complex systems that run our modern world require the coordination of people, equipment, energy, material and information. 

A master’s degree in industrial and systems engineering prepares you with the skills and knowledge you need to bring logistics and order to an increasingly connected and chaotic world.  

At UofL, you can customize your degree to match your career goals with focus areas in:

  • Human factors
  • Data analytics and operations research
  • Logistics and supply chain

You can also earn your master's in industrial and systems engineering by progressively building your knowledge through UofL's online Stackable Engineering Certificates pathway.

Is UofL’s Online MSISE Program the Right One for You?

The online MSISE program accepts students with undergraduate degrees in both engineering and non-engineering disciplines. Unlike many graduate programs, our online master of science in industrial and systems engineering program at the University of Louisville does not require GRE test scores for admission. 

Our admission process focuses on each applicant's potential to succeed and make an impact as an industrial and systems engineer. If you have a technical degree or relevant experience and a passion for industrial and systems engineering, our program could be a great fit for you.

What Are Admissions Requirements for the Online Master’s in Industrial and Systems Engineering?

To be considered for admission, applicants must have:

  • A bachelor’s degree or its equivalent from an accredited institution
    • Applicants with a baccalaureate degree other than Industrial and Systems Engineering should consult with the Director of the MS program in the Department of Industrial and Systems Engineering on their readiness and necessary remedial work in order to succeed.
  • A minimum undergraduate GPA of 2.75 on a 4.0 scale (applicants who do not meet the minimum GPA may inquire about conditional acceptance)

*Required application packet materials include:

  • Transcripts showing Calculus I or higher
  • Two letters of recommendation
  • Current resume

*Your application portal:

Once you have started the graduate application, you can check the status and review any additional checklist to-do items. Log in to your application using the email address you used to apply for admission and your password. Your checklist items may include additional materials or documentation that facilitate a smooth admissions process. You will also have access to important contact information and next steps after an admissions decision is made.
 

Online Master of Science in Industrial and Systems Engineering International Applicants

The master of science in industrial engineering program is open to international students. If you live outside of the United States (U.S.) and intend to complete an online academic program from your home country, be sure to review these additional requirements for international students. International students will not be issued a U.S. visa if admitted to an online program since there are no campus attendance requirements.

  • All international transcripts must be certified as authentic and translated verbatim in English.
  • Students whose primary language is not English must show English language proficiency by attaining one of the following:
    • TOEFL scores of 79 or higher on the internet-based test OR
    • Duolingo English test score of 105 or higher OR
    • English proficiency can also be met by submitting official IELTS scores of at least 6.5 overall band score from the academic module exam

What Will You Learn in the Online Master of Science in Industrial and Systems Engineering?

The MS in industrial and systems engineering curriculum blends analytical rigor with applied systems thinking. Core coursework builds advanced competency in probability, statistics and experimental design, while specialized focus areas allow you to deepen expertise in analytics, supply chain optimization or human-centered systems engineering. Whether you pursue a thesis, project or course-only pathway, you will graduate prepared to solve complex operational challenges using data-driven methods.

Online Courses

The online MSISE is a 30-credit-hour (10 course) program with course options that allow you to pursue your interests in areas spanning from management or communications to geography. The program is 100% online and courses are delivered asynchronously, in standard term length. 

Choose from thesis, project or course-only options to align your degree with your professional goals.

To see all available online courses and degree requirements, please visit UofL's Academic Catalog page (select Degree Requirements tab).

What Else Should You Know?

The questions and answers below help you learn more about our program, delivery method, application and admission, internship process and options or how to succeed as an online student at UofL and beyond. 

  • My bachelor’s degree is not in engineering. Can I still apply to earn the online industrial and systems engineering?

Yes. The minimum requirement for admission is a baccalaureate degree or its equivalent from an accredited institution. We welcome applicants with undergraduate degree from diverse areas or background such as business, psychology, political science, mathematics, chemistry, physics or computer science. In some cases, remedial work may be required and can be accomplished in advance by taking course(s) either at UofL or at recommended community colleges. For instance, students who do not come from a STEM field will need to have Calculus I to be admitted into this program.

Applicants with a baccalaureate degree other than Industrial and Systems Engineering should consult with the Director of the MS program in the Department of Industrial and Systems Engineering on their readiness and necessary remedial work in order to succeed.

  • What are the academic prerequisites for successful applicants to the online master's in industrial and systems engineering program?

The industrial and systems engineering department requires all applicants complete the Calculus I class prior to application/admission to the MSISE program. You can complete this prerequisite as a non-degree student at UofL or elsewhere.

At UofL, you can take: Math180 - Elements of Calculus - QR, OR Math 205 - Calculus I – QR. Check the Schedule of Courses for more information. You can also satisfy the Calculus I requirement at Straighterline.com.

  • Is GRE/GMAT score required?

No, you do not need to submit GRE/GMAT scores.

  • I am an international student. Can I apply to the MSISE program?

Yes. Please note, however, that international students will not be issued a U.S. visa, if admitted to the MSISE online program since there are no campus attendance requirements.

  • When do I need to send my official transcripts?

Official transcripts are required as a part of a complete application at the time of application for full consideration. Unofficial transcripts, with degree posted, can be accepted until official transcripts are received.

  • Can I transfer graduate courses completed at another institution to UofL’s online master’s in industrial and systems engineering program?

Yes. Students may request to transfer up to six (6) credit hours from another accredited graduate program.

  • Does the ISE department offer financial aid to incoming online MSISE students?

Scholarship opportunities may exist for exceptional applicants contingent upon availability of funding. These limited scholarships, designed to partially offset the tuition, are determined on a rolling basis at the time of admission. Official offer letters to individual students will contain details.

  • What are the eligibility requirements and timeline for receiving the Department of Industrial and Systems Engineering merit-based scholarship?

The merit-based scholarship is designed to partially offset the tuition for classes that a student is taking. Students who wish to apply for scholarship must submit a one-page statement of purpose demonstrating qualifications and intent towards the program. 

Demonstrated strengths will be assessed in a holistic manner with focus on, but not limited to, current work profile and future goals, prior industry experience (internships/co-op, full-time), accomplishments in prior academic work (e.g., undergraduate, certificates), plans for successfully completing the degree, applying knowledge to practice and advocating for IE professionals.

  • When can I expect to receive the admission decision?

You will be informed within four weeks of the Graduate School receiving your complete application.

  • Is an online degree accepted in industrial and systems engineering?

Yes, online industrial and systems engineering degrees are fully accepted and accredited just like on-campus programs, providing the same quality education and career opportunities.

  • How long does an online master's degree in industrial and systems engineering take?

A typical online master's in industrial and systems engineering takes one to two years to complete, with flexibility to adjust your pace and take time off if needed.

  • What's the difference between industrial and systems engineering and engineering management?

industrial and systems engineering focuses more on optimizing complex systems and processes, while engineering management emphasizes leading technical teams and engineering operations
