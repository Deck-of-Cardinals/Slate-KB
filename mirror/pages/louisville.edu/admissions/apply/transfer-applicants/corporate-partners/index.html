Discover how the University of Louisville partners with local and global industry leaders to provide educational benefits to employees and dependents.

See our Current Corporate Partners

Amazon Career Choice

Amazon Career Choice is an education benefit that empowers employees to learn new skills for career success. Employees have the opportunity to study at their own pace while earning credits toward an associate’s degree, bachelor’s degree or academic certificate.

THRIVE BY 5 LOUISVILLE

Thrive By 5 Louisville is a non-profit organization committed to making high-quality early childhood education accessible across Louisville by strengthening the early learning workforce, supporting providers and removing barriers for families and educators. Thrive By 5 builds partnerships and resources that help early learning thrive citywide.

Future Ready Scholarship: 

  • Covers last-dollar tuition, books and fees.
  • Available to current and prospective UofL students in all majors excluding Early Childhood Education.
  • Students will work at a participating early learning location to qualify. Students must be new to the ECE workforce.
  • Childcare at an early learning location is also provided to employees and scholarship recipients.
  • Contact info@thriveby5louisville.org for more information. 

UpLift Fund: 

  • Provides assistance to individuals working in early childhood education who are facing barriers to enrollment or completion (for example, books, past-due balance, technology needs, etc). Students must be pursuing a degree or credential in Early Childhood Education.
  • Designated to remove financial obstacles and support continued progress in education and credentialing.

UofL Health

Careers in accounting/finance, administration, healthcare, information technology, nursing and more. 

Benefits:
