Important News

Fall 26 charges will begin to show in the ULink Student System on July 1, 2026 . Due date for the Fall 26 term is August 21, 2026. 

Summer 26 charges will begin to show in the ULink Student System on April 15, 2026. Due date for the Summer 26 term is July 8, 2026.

Make a Payment

Visit ULink > Student > Financial Account > Make A Payment

  • Payments from your checking account
    • No fee when paying with this option
  • Credit/Debit card payments
    • We accept Discover, Mastercard and VISA
    • There is a 2.75% non-refundable transaction fee to use credit/debit cards

If you have any questions, please reach out to the Bursar's office at 502-852-6503 or by email at bursar@louisville.edu

Email notifications from the Bursar

The official method of communication with the University of Louisville Bursar's Office is via the university email service account (bursar@louisville.edu).

  • In order to stay informed and aware, students are required to set up and maintain their email accounts.
  • Check your email frequently.
  • NO paper billing statements will be issued.
  • It is recommended that you add the following email address to your "safe" list within your email system's spam filters: bursar@louisville.edu
  • If our office needs to mail a hard copy of a check, document, form or notice, we will use the mailing address from your ULink account.
