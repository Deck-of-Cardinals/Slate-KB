UofL offers certificate programs for professionals who need to expand their skills and knowledge beyond their bachelor's degree or who need to add valuable credentials to their current or future job and career path.

The following certificates are available:

  • Accountancy Certificate - College of Business (4-year degree required)
  • Accountancy Certificate Online Program - College of Business (4-year degree required)
  • Climate, Equity and Opportunity in the Workplace Certificate - College of Education and Human Development
  • Construction Operations Certificate - Speed School of Engineering
  • Equine Business Certificate - College of Business
  • Ethics Certificate - College of Arts and Sciences (4-year degree required)
  • Franchise Management Certificate - College of Business
  • Geospatial Technologies, Applied Certificate - College of Arts and Sciences
  • Geospatial Technologies, Applied Certificate Online Program - College of Arts and Sciences
  • Healthcare Leadership Certificate - College of Education and Human Development
  • Healthcare Leadership Certificate Online Program - College of Education and Human Development
  • Human Resource Leadership Certificate - College of Education and Human Development
  • Human Resource Leadership Certificate Online Program - College of Education and Human Development
  • LGBTQ Health Studies Certificate - College of Arts & Sciences
  • Peace, Justice and Conflict Transformation Certificate - College of Arts & Sciences
  • Police Executive Leadership Development Certificate - College of Arts & Sciences
  • Sales Certificate - College of Business

To Apply:

  1. Complete the admissions application.
  2. Pay your $30 application fee.
  3. Submit your official degree-showing transcript(s). If you received your bachelor's degree from the University of Louisville, you do not need to submit a transcript.
