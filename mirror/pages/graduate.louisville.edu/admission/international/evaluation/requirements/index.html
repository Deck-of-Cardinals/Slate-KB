Credential Evaluation Guidelines

To ensure your credential evaluation meets our requirements for admission, enrollment, and transfer of credit, please follow these guidelines:

  1. 1
    Official evaluations and transcripts must be sent directly from your chosen NACES or AICE credential evaluation company (see below) to our University.

    For admissions purposes, unofficial copies of evaluations and transcripts are acceptable. However, please note that some departments may require an official external international credential evaluation before reviewing your application. It’s important to confirm specific requirements with the respective department to ensure you submit all necessary documentation.

    Review the Steps to Submit Your Evaluation about
  2. 2
    Ensure your evaluation includes all required documents

    All credential evaluations must be accompanied by the transcripts used to generate them.
