Welcome to the Office of Undergraduate Advising

We are excited to meet you! Our academic advisors are here to help you make educational plans, explore options, get involved, develop community, and find the resources you need for success. You are the expert on you! And we want to get to know you, so that we can support you in the exact ways you need. 

We hope that you'll come see your advisor early and often. If you have questions or concerns, please come ask us! Or maybe you just want to brag about how well your semester is going—we love to celebrate with you, too! 

We strongly recommend you meet with your advisor at least once a semester. Some academic units and majors require you to have an advising appointment before you can register for the next semester. Even if it's not required for you, please come see us. We're all advisors because we love our students, and we want to know how you are doing!
