Set up a PIN

Note: The PIN is used to validate identity when contacting our office via telephone. Individuals contacting the Bursar's office on your behalf will need the PIN number information.

If you cannot remember your PIN, delete the stored information and create a new one. It will be ready to use immediately upon saving.

  1. 1
    Log in into your ULink account.
    Sign in to ULink about
  2. 2
    Click on the Profile icon, then click on PIN.
  3. 3
    Enter a 6-digit numeric PIN in the box.
  4. 4
    Enter the same PIN in second box.

    Note: Entries must match.

  5. 5
    Click Save

    Now your PIN is ready to use.
