Master of Science in Physiology, Online

The online Master of Science in Physiology (MSP) at the University of Louisville is designed for aspiring health care professionals looking to bolster their medical-science knowledge and expertise for career advancement in healthcare or medical research, as preparation for a terminal degree in medical sciences (i.e., doctorate of medicine, doctorate of dental surgery, doctorate of dental medicine, doctorate of optometry, physician assistant) or for employment in medical-related fields.

Unlike traditional medical-science programs that focus on lectures followed by memorization and assessment, our unique and fully online curriculum emphasizes comprehension and application of physiologic concepts. 

Our adaptive learning approach helps students prepare for success in clinically-based careers as they progress along their professional and academic journey. It provides the tools and support needed for aspiring medical school students in the U.S. or international students seeking to strengthen their medical-science credentials.

The master’s in physiology online program is offered through the School of Medicine at the University of Louisville, a leader in medical research and patient care, where faculty and students identify and reach new frontiers in medical training and innovation.

The Big Picture

The online Master of Science in Physiology at the University of Louisville provides advanced scientific training for students in Kentucky, across the nation and even abroad, who are pursuing careers in health care, biomedical research and related professional programs. 

Designed to strengthen foundational and applied knowledge of human physiology, UofL’s online graduate physiology program emphasizes integrative systems, evidence-based analysis and scientific reasoning. This degree is ideal for students preparing for medical, dental, physical therapy or other health professions, as well as those seeking advancement in research or clinical support roles.

Online MS in Physiology Degree Highlights 

  • Advance your knowledge in medical-related sciences with a broad, interdisciplinary curriculum designed by expert faculty at UofL’s School of Medicine
  • Develop critical thinking and analytical skills through evidence-based coursework
  • Increase the likelihood of admission to a professional-degree program or likelihood of employment in medical-related for-profit companies or other medical-related career opportunities through advanced, graduate-level studies in biomedical applications, biophysics, clinical physiology and more
  • Balance your studies with work and personal commitments with our flexible, 100% online education
  • Earn your degree from a nationally recognized academic and R1 research institution
  • Broaden your career opportunities through career advice from our School of Medicine Department of Physiology faculty who have trained thousands of past students

Online MS in Physiology Admissions Requirements and Materials

To be considered for admission, applicants must have:

  • A bachelor’s degree or its equivalent from an accredited institution
  • A minimum undergraduate GPA of 2.8 on a 4.0 scale (applicants who do not meet the minimum GPA may inquire about conditional acceptance)

*Required application packet materials include:

  • A one-page personal statement discussing why you desire a MS degree in Physiology
  • Two letters of recommendation from people who are well acquainted with your previous academic work

*Your Application Portal:

Once you have started the graduate application, you can check the status and review any additional checklist to-do items. Log in to your application using the email address you used to apply for admission and your password. Your checklist items may include additional materials or documentation that facilitate a smooth admissions process. You will also have access to important contact information and next steps after an admissions decision is made.
 

Online MS in Physiology for International Students

The Master of Science in Physiology online program is open to international students. If you live outside of the United States and intend to complete an online academic program from your home country, be sure to review these additional admission requirements for international students.

  • International students will not be issued a U.S. visa, since there are no campus attendance requirements. 
    All courses in the program are taught 100% online, allowing you to attain the entire degree from the comfort of your home and at any time convenient for you, regardless of your current location, anywhere in the world. Additionally, in the case of a foreign applicant from a non-English speaking country, the applicant must achieve a TOEFL Examination overall score of 4.0, with minimum sub-scores of 4.0 in each skill area.

What Can I Do With an Online MS in Physiology?  

Graduates of the MS in Physiology are prepared for a wide range of health science and research-focused career paths. The program builds the advanced scientific knowledge and analytical skills required for success in professional health programs, clinical support roles and biomedical research environments. With growing demand for skilled health professionals and researchers, a master’s degree in physiology provides a competitive advantage for both immediate career advancement and continued academic study.

According to the Bureau of Labor Statistics, employment for medical scientists is projected to grow 11%, much faster than average, by 2033. The median annual wage for clinical researchers was approximately $60,000 as of May 2021. With advanced education and experience, salaries can exceed $90,000, especially in specialized roles (bls.gov).

Some of the specific jobs for professionals with a master’s in physiology program may include:

  • Clinical Technician/Lab Technician (2024 median salary: $61,890)
  • Clinical Research Coordinator (2024 median salary: $61,890)
  • Medical Product Specialist (2024 median salary: $117,960)
  • Medical Researcher (2024 median salary: $100,590)
  • Healthcare Consultant (2024 median salary: $101,190)
Source: bls/gov

Career Opportunities for MS in Physiology Graduates

Graduates of this program are well-suited for roles in medical product development, pharmaceuticals and medical device firms, private practices (such as optometry) and medical laboratories.

Career Pathways Include:

  • Medical, dental and professional health programs oversight
  • Biomedical or clinical research

If you seek a career or advancement in biomedical research, the pharmaceutical industry or health sciences, this master’s program can provide you with essential foundational knowledge, setting you up for success in your profession. 

If you plan on pursuing medical school or other health-related professional programs, the online MS in Physiology can enhance your academic qualifications in the biomedical sciences before you apply. 

Whichever pathway you choose, you will join a highly regarded and internationally recognized program.

What Else Should You Know?

The questions and answers below help you learn more about our program, courses, delivery or how to succeed as an online student at UofL and beyond. 

  • Is the online MS in Physiology right for me?

The online MS in Physiology is an excellent choice if you seek to strengthen your academic credentials in the biomedical sciences before applying to medical school or other health-related professional programs. It also serves as an ideal standalone master’s degree if you need foundational knowledge to advance your career in biomedical research, the pharmaceutical industry or the health sciences.

  • Can I take core physiology courses without earning a degree? 

Yes, you can apply as a non-degree graduate student and take up to 15 credit hours. Non-degree students may pay a different rate than fully online degree students. Please review the published tuition rates for more information.

If you decide to pursue the master’s degree, you will be required to submit a new application for the MS in Physiology program. Credits earned as a non-degree student will be reviewed by the physiology department upon admission to the program. For more information, please contact Jennifer Wells in the Department of Physiology at jennifer.wells@louisville.edu or 502.852.6231.

  • What does the Adaptive Learning Model entail? 

Our approach to teaching and learning aims to personalize students’ academic experience allowing them to interact with the course material and build upon their foundational knowledge. Students can flex their learning style, demonstrate the knowledge they gained and spend more time on the knowledge they still need to acquire through additional course materials.

  • What is the format of the online program? 

The graduate physiology program is fully online, allowing for flexible scheduling. Courses are delivered asynchronously via Blackboard in 8-week terms.

  • Are there any on-campus requirements? 

There are no on-campus requirements for the completion of the master's in physiology online degree.

  • How long does it take to complete the program? 

The program is 30 credit hours and could be completed full-time over 12 months (over three semesters). However, most students are working professionals enrolled part-time, taking 6 credit hours per semester (1 course every 8 weeks) and aiming to finish the program within 2-3 years.
